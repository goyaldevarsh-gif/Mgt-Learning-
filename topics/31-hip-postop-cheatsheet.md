## 1. Post-op management [[p.321-323]]

- No universal protocol. **Pre-op teaching session** helps
- Posture: ~**15 deg abduction**; posterior approach: triangular pillow (loose straps, **peroneal nerve**); DAA: ordinary pillow
- Day 0: ankle pumps, quadriceps and gluteal isometrics, gentle rotation, gait training. **Straight leg raise NOT helpful after THA** (groin pain, rotational stress on stem)
- Weight bearing: **cemented = tolerance**; cementless ingrowth = 6-8 wks limited by some, others early (Hol: no harm; but protect on stairs: torsional loads); structural graft / revision: crutches ~**3 months**
- Discharge when: bed transfers, level walking, few steps
- **Hip precautions questioned:** meta-analysis (1122 pts), unrestricted patients more satisfied, return faster, **no increase in dislocation**
- Self-directed PT: about 1/3 needed formal PT (Klement)
- First 6 weeks: elevated toilet seat, pillow between knees, showers not baths. Drive at **2-4 wks**
- Abductor weakness persists to **1 year** (Foucher)
- Work: sedentary 4 wks; limited lifting 2-3 months; **no manual labour**
- **OK:** swimming, cycling, golf. **Avoid:** jogging, racquet sports (impact)
- Follow-up: **2 wks, 6 wks, 3 months, 1 year**, then periodic. Routine X-ray **every 2 years** (loosening, wear, osteolysis can be asymptomatic)

## 2. Eight tough concepts

> [!samajh] **Offset**
> Offset badhane se abductor ka lever lamba, load kam, impingement kam. Length badhaye bina offset badhana ho to medial neck attachment / high-offset stem.

> [!samajh] **Cup position**
> Inclination 40 +/- 10, anteversion 15 +/- 10 sirf starting point. **Stiff spine = zyada anteversion + inclination. Flatback/hypermobile = kam.** Cup + stem combined anteversion ~35 (25-50).

> [!samajh] **Dislocation direction**
> Anterior = cup ya stem zyada anteverted. Posterior = retroverted ya capsule/rotators repair nahi. Posterior repair se dislocation 4.46% se 0.4%.

> [!samajh] **Stress shielding**
> Stem diameter^4 stiffness. Bada, CoCr, extensively coated = zyada proximal bone loss. Revision me bone stock ka problem.

> [!samajh] **Femur Dorr and stems**
> A (champagne flute) cemented/cementless dono. C (stovepipe, old women) = cemented ya careful. > 75 yrs registry me cemented better.

> [!samajh] **PJI decision**
> Early / acute haematogenous + stable = DAIR + liner/head change. Chronic ya loose = implant out, two-stage (3 months). Sinus, unknown/virulent organism = two-stage.

> [!samajh] **Acetabular defects**
> Cavitary = graft + oversize cup. Segmental large = augment ya structural graft (>= 50% host support). Discontinuity = plate/distraction/cup-cage/triflange.

> [!samajh] **Femoral defects (Paprosky)**
> Isthmus > 4 cm = extensively coated/fluted. < 4 cm = impaction grafting / fluted tapered. Nothing = composite / megaprosthesis.
