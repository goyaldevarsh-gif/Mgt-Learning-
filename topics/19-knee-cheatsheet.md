Last-minute revision of Campbell Ch.7 Knee. **Concepts = meri taraf se. Numbers = book se, page ke saath.**

## 1. Seven tough concepts

> [!samajh] **PS box, cam, post**
> Femur mein beech ka window = **box**; uske peeche ki crossbar = **cam**; tibial poly ka peg = **post**. ~70 deg pe cam post pe takrata hai aur femur ko peeche kheenchta hai = **rollback**. Fayda: flexion. Nuksan: post pe stress/wear, interface pe load.

> [!samajh] **Patellar clunk**
> Quadriceps tendon ke peeche ka nodule flexion mein box mein fasta hai, 30-45 deg extension pe jhatke se nikalta hai. Fix: longer groove, arthroscopic debridement.

> [!samajh] **Gaps**
> Distal femur = extension. Posterior femur = flexion. Tibia = dono. PCL release = flexion gap +4-6 mm.

> [!samajh] **Femoral rotation**
> Tibia perpendicular kati (asli tibia 3 deg varus). Femur ~3 deg ER = rectangular flexion gap. Valgus/hypoplastic lateral condyle = epicondylar / Whiteside axis.

> [!samajh] **PCL tether**
> Contracted PCL rassi ki tarah deformity pakde rakhti hai. Varus CR knee mein PCL recess karo; PS mein pehle hi nikal chuki hoti hai.

> [!samajh] **Roll-forward (CR)**
> Flexion mein medial condyle aage slide kare to anterior lip / deep-dish.

> [!samajh] **Infection decision**
> < 4 wks ya acute haematogenous + well-fixed = DAIR + poly exchange. Chronic = implant out: two-stage (6 wks IV, titer 1:8), fusion (IM nail 95%) ya resection.
