## 1. Basics
- Pain at the coccyx, **worse on sitting and on rising from sitting**, relieved by standing or walking.
- **Women 3-5x more common.** Obese patients get it mostly from **posterior tilt**, thin patients from **direct bone pressure** (ischial support kam hota hai).
- **Anatomy:** coccyx = 3-5 fused/semi-fused segments (Co1-Co4). Attachments: **levator ani, coccygeus, gluteus maximus** fibres, anococcygeal ligament. Isliye pelvic floor spasm bhi pain deta hai.
- Normal sitting mein coccyx **anteriorly flex** hota hai. Ye movement abnormal ho (zyada ya bilkul nahi) to pain aata hai.

## 2. Causes
| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Traumatic** | Fall on buttock (most common), childbirth (prolonged/instrumental), repeated microtrauma (cycling, rowing) |
| **Non-traumatic / idiopathic** | Obesity, hypermobility, bony spicule, degenerative changes, prolonged sitting |
| **Referred (rule out)** | Lumbar disc/facet, sacroiliac, pelvic floor dysfunction, piriformis, anorectal (proctalgia fugax, abscess, fissure), pilonidal sinus, gynae/urological causes |
| **Serious (rare)** | **Chordoma, metastasis, infection**, sacral insufficiency fracture |

## 3. Classification: static shape vs dynamic findings

> [!flag] Correction: pehle jo "Postacchini-Maigne Type I-IV" likha tha wo standard numbering nahi hai. Do alag classifications mix ho gayi thi. Viva ya paper mein "Type I, II..." mat bolo, seedha findings bolo.

**1. Postacchini & Massobrio: static X-ray shape (Type I-IV)**
Ye sirf coccyx ki shape pe based hai (jitna mujhe yaad hai):
- **Type I:** coccyx halka sa aage curved, tip neeche ki taraf.
- **Type II:** zyada curved, tip seedha aage ki taraf.
- **Type III:** sharp angulation (do segments ke beech mein).
- **Type IV:** sacrococcygeal ya intercoccygeal **subluxation**.

Kuch authors isme aur types ya bone spicule alag se jodte hain. Isliye jahan "Type V" likha mile, wahan source check karo ki kis system ki baat hai.

**2. Maigne: dynamic (sitting vs standing) findings**
Ye numbered types nahi, **findings** hain:
- **Hypermobility** (zyada anterior flexion)
- **Posterior luxation/subluxation** (sitting mein)
- **Bone spicule**
- **Normal mobility**

Maine inhe "Type I (normal), II (hypermobile), III (luxation), IV (spicule)" bol diya tha. Wo mera banaya hua simplification tha, kisi book ka official numbering nahi.

**Practical matlab:** viva ya paper mein "Type I, II..." mat bolo. Seedha **findings** bolo: normal mobility, hypermobility, luxation, spicule. Surgery ka logic wahi rehta hai: **objective abnormality** (hypermobility, luxation, spicule) ho to coccygectomy ka case banta hai, normal mobility mein kam.

Exact classification ke liye Postacchini & Massobrio (JBJS Am 1983) aur Maigne ke papers dekh lo. Chaho to main search karke verify kar deta hoon.

**Type 5 aur 6 ke baare mein:**
Mujhe in dono ki definition pakki yaad nahi hai. Kuch later papers ya reviews ne classification mein types add kiye hain (jaise Nathan et al. ki morphology wali study), par main exact kya hai ye guess nahi karunga, kyunki galat definition viva mein nuksan karegi.

**Practical:** jo source tumne dekha hai (book ya paper), usme Type 5/6 ki definition dekh ke mujhe bhej do, main use baaki findings ke saath samjha dunga. Ya bolo to main search karke original papers (Postacchini 1983, Maigne 2000, Nathan 2010) verify kar deta hoon, aur proper classification table bana deta hoon.

## 4. Assessment

**History:** onset (fall/childbirth?), pain on sitting, on getting up, defecation/intercourse pain, duration, treatment tried.

**Red flags (investigate pehle):** night pain, weight loss, fever, progressive pain without trauma, bowel/bladder/sensory changes, history of malignancy. Inme MRI zaroor.

**Exam:**
1. **Inspection:** skin, sinus tract ya dimple (pilonidal). Lumbar, SI joint aur hip bhi dekho.
2. **Palpation (external):** coccyx tip aur sacrococcygeal joint tender hai.
3. **Intrarectal exam (sabse important, par sirf agar patient comfortable ho aur consent ho):**
   - Coccyx ko thumb (bahar) aur index finger (andar) se pakdo.
   - Move karke dekho: **hypermobile, fixed, ya painful with movement.**
   - **Levator ani/coccygeus spasm** (tender, taut band).
   - Mass ya fissure bhi check ho jaata hai.
4. Lumbar exam, SLR, SI tests, piriformis. Agar ye pain reproduce karte hain to referred pain mano.
5. Neuro exam (saddle sensation, perianal reflex).

## 5. Imaging
- **Dynamic lateral X-ray (sitting + standing):** sabse useful. Dono mein coccyx ke angle ki tulna karo. Isse normal mobility, hypermobility, luxation ya spicule decide hota hai.
- **Standing lateral:** acute trauma mein fracture/dislocation ke liye.
- **MRI:** red flags, doubt, ya X-ray normal aur symptoms atypical hon. Tumor, infection, nerve root aur pelvic pathology dikhata hai.
- **CT:** rare, fracture detail ya surgical planning.
- **Note:** **sirf imaging pe diagnosis mat banao.** Clinical examination aur dynamic X-ray dono mila ke dekho, kyunki imaging mein "changes" asymptomatic logo mein bhi milte hain.

## 6. Acute coccydynia (recent fall, <~3 months)
Suspect: **contusion, fracture, ya sacrococcygeal dislocation.**

1. Lateral X-ray (standing, agar tolerate ho). Fracture/dislocation dhoondo.
2. **Mostly conservative:**
   - **NSAIDs** short course, paracetamol.
   - **Cushion:** **wedge-shaped ya donut/cut-out cushion** jisme coccyx ke neeche jagah ho. Isse pressure ischial tuberosities pe jaata hai.
   - **Sitting posture:** thoda aage jhuk ke baitho, ek taraf ya forward-tilted pelvis.
   - Prolonged sitting avoid, baar baar uthna.
   - Heat/ice, local application.
   - **Stool softener/laxative** (constipation aur straining coccyx pe zor deta hai).
3. **Dislocation/displaced fracture:** kuch cases mein manual reduction (sedation/local, intrarectal manoeuvre) try hota hai, par evidence limited hai aur recurrence ho sakta hai. Most cases conservative treatment pe settle ho jaate hain.
4. Review **2-4 weeks** baad. Zyada tar patients **6-8 weeks** mein theek ho jaate hain.

**Counsel patient:** "Hadi chhoti hai par heal hone mein time lagta hai. Cushion aur dawai se 80-90% theek ho jaate hain." (Number rough hai, flag.)

## 7. Chronic coccydynia (>2-3 months)
Stepwise, kam invasive se zyada invasive:

**Step 1: Conservative optimisation**
- Cushion check (sahi use ho raha hai?), posture, weight reduction (obese patients mein).
- **Pelvic floor physio:** levator ani relaxation, stretching, **intrarectal massage/manual therapy** (Thiele massage), biofeedback.
- NSAIDs short courses, **neuropathic agents** (amitriptyline, gabapentinoids) jab burning/neuropathic component ho.
- Heat, TENS, ultrasound (evidence weak).

**Step 2: Injections** (ek ya do baar)
- **Local anaesthetic + steroid injection** coccyx tip ya sacrococcygeal joint pe (palpation ya fluoroscopy guide). Studies mein success ~**60-85%** report hota hai, par long-term relief kam hota hai (flag).
- **Ganglion impar block:** sympathetic ganglion (sacrococcygeal junction ke samne), fluoroscopy ya USG guided. Local anaesthetic + steroid. Mixed evidence, achha option agar sympathetic-type pain ho.
- **Radiofrequency ablation/neurolysis of ganglion impar** agar block se temporary relief mile.
- **ESWT (shock wave):** kuch studies mein useful, evidence limited.

**Step 3: Surgery, coccygectomy (last resort)**
- **Kab:** **>6 months failed** conservative aur injections, severe disability, aur **dynamic X-ray mein objective abnormality** (hypermobility, luxation, spicule).
- **Kya:** partial ya total coccygectomy. Total mein ligament sacrococcygeal sheet se alag karke coccyx nikalte hain, soft tissue repair karte hain.
- **Results:** reported success ~**70-90%**, par patient selection pe heavily depend karta hai.
- **Complications:** **wound infection/dehiscence** (10-20% tak reported hai kyunki anus paas hai, flag), persistent pain, rectal injury, CSF leak (rare).
- **Poor prognostic factors:** normal mobility (koi objective abnormality nahi), **psychological comorbidity (depression, litigation/compensation)**, multiple prior failed treatments, obesity.
- **Pre-op:** positive diagnostic injection (block se relief) surgical outcome predict karta hai. Bowel prep, perioperative antibiotics.

## 8. Quick decision flow
1. **Red flag?** → MRI/workup, ye coccydynia nahi ho sakta.
2. **Acute (<3 mo, fall):** X-ray lateral → NSAIDs + cushion + posture + laxative. Follow-up 4 weeks.
3. **Chronic:** dynamic X-ray + intrarectal exam → findings decide karo (normal mobility, hypermobility, luxation, spicule).
4. Normal mobility: conservative + physio + injections, **surgery avoid**.
5. Hypermobility / luxation / spicule: injection try karo, fail ho to **coccygectomy** discuss karo.
6. Hamesha **lumbar/SI/pelvic floor/anorectal** referred pain rule out karo.

## 9. Counselling (OPD mein bolne wali baatein)
- Ye zyada tar **months** mein theek hota hai, jaldi nahi.
- Cushion regularly use karo, lamba na baitho.
- Constipation avoid karo.
- Pain ka mana jaata hai, par serious nahi hota (red flag nahi hai to).
- Surgery last option hai, aur uske risk bhi hain.

## 10. OPD rules {revise}
1. **Sitting se pain, standing se relief** = coccydynia pattern.
2. **Dynamic X-ray + intrarectal exam**, dono zaroori.
3. Acute = **conservative**, almost sab theek ho jaate hain.
4. Chronic = **stepwise**, injection pehle, surgery last.
5. **Surgery sirf objective abnormality** (hypermobility, luxation, spicule) mein.
6. Hamesha red flags aur referred causes dhoondo.

## Samajh: coccyx ka normal aur abnormal movement

**Idea:** Coccyx ek fixed haddi nahi hai, sacrococcygeal joint pe thodi hinge ki tarah move karta hai. Normal movement ka matlab hai ki wo sitting ke time **thoda aage (anterior) jhuk jaata hai**, aur uthne pe wapas seedha ho jaata hai.

**Normal sitting mein kya hota hai:**
- Baithte ho to body weight ischial tuberosities (sit bones) pe aata hai, aur pelvis thoda peeche tilt hota hai.
- Isse coccyx ka tip chair ki taraf aata hai, to uspe pressure aata hai, aur wo **aage ki taraf flex** ho jaata hai (tip ka angle thoda change hota hai).
- Ye chhota movement pressure ko absorb kar leta hai, jaise shock absorber. Normally isse dard nahi hota.
- Is flexion ki range alag logon mein alag hoti hai, isliye "normal" ka ek fixed number nahi hai.

**Jab ye movement abnormal ho:**

1. **Zyada flexion (hypermobility):**
   - Sacrococcygeal joint ya ligaments loose hain, to coccyx zarurat se zyada aage jhuk jaata hai.
   - Joint pe bar bar excess stress aata hai, to dard hota hai (sitting pe).
   - Dynamic X-ray mein sitting vs standing mein angle ka difference bahut zyada dikhta hai (Maigne ka figure ~25° se upar, flag).

2. **Zyada peeche subluxation :**
   - Sitting mein joint pe coccyx peeche ya aage khisak jaata hai (luxation/subluxation).
   - Standing mein wapas aa sakta hai, ya fixed bhi ho sakta hai.
   - Ye instability bhi pain deti hai.

3. **Bilkul movement nahi (stiff/fixed ya spicule):**
   - Coccyx fused ya stiff hai, ya bone spicule hai jo andar ki taraf nukila hai.
   - Sitting mein flex nahi ho paata, to **pressure absorb nahi hota** aur direct soft tissue pe jaata hai.
   - Spicule hai to sitting mein wo tissue ko chubhta hai.
   - Ye normal-looking X-ray pe bhi ho sakta hai, isliye dynamic X-ray zaroori hai.

**Ek analogy:** Gaadi ki suspension. Normal suspension jhatka absorb karta hai. Zyada loose ho to bahut hilta hai (hypermobility), bilkul jam ho to jhatka seedha body pe aata hai (stiff). Dono mein problem hai.

**Isliye dynamic X-ray lete hain:**
Sitting aur standing lateral X-ray mein coccyx ka angle compare karte hain. Isse pata chalta hai ki movement normal hai, zyada hai, ya hai hi nahi, aur kya luxation ya spicule hai. Yahi decide karta hai ki surgery se fayda hoga ya nahi.

**Caveat:** Ye abnormal movement kai asymptomatic logon mein bhi mil sakti hai. Isliye sirf X-ray dekh ke diagnose mat karo, clinical exam aur intrarectal palpation ke saath mila ke dekho.

## Maigne's dynamic findings (sitting vs standing)

Maigne ne dynamic lateral X-rays (**sitting vs standing**) se coccyx ko dekha. Ye 2000 ke Spine paper ka core idea hai (Maigne, Doursounian, Chatellier). Neeche exact numbers memory se hain, isliye flag kiye hain.

**Setup:** standing lateral aur sitting lateral (coccyx pe weight ke saath). Dono mein sacrococcygeal angle aur coccyx ki position compare karte hain.

### 1. Normal mobility
- Standing se sitting mein coccyx thoda **anterior flex** hota hai (chhota change).
- Koi displacement nahi, koi spicule nahi.
- Aise patients mein dard ka cause aksar coccyx ke bahar dhoondhna padta hai (pelvic floor, referred pain). Surgery se fayda kam.

### 2. Hypermobility (excessive anterior flexion)
- Sitting mein coccyx **zarurat se zyada aage flex** hota hai.
- Maigne ka cutoff **~25° se zyada flexion** hai (exact number alag papers mein alag hai, verify kar lena).
- **Meaning:** joint ya ligaments loose hain, to sitting ke time sacrococcygeal joint pe excess stress aata hai.

### 3. Posterior luxation (ya subluxation)
- Sitting mein coccyx sacrum ke relative **peeche ki taraf khisak** jaata hai.
- Standing mein wapas aa sakta hai, isliye **sirf sitting film se hi pakda jaata hai**. Static standing X-ray normal dikh sakta hai.
- **Meaning:** joint unstable hai, sitting ke weight pe displace hota hai.
- Mujhe pakka yaad hai ki Maigne ne hypermobility aur luxation dono ko milake **"instability"** bola. Luxation ke liye numerical cutoff mujhe pakka nahi yaad, to guess nahi karunga.

### 4. Bone spicule
- Coccyx ke tip ya dorsal side pe **nukila bony projection**.
- Sitting mein ye **soft tissue mein chubhta** hai, to dard hota hai.
- Lateral X-ray pe dikhta hai, par chhota spicule easily miss ho sakta hai.

### Is paper ke aur important points (as far as I remember)
- **Obese patients (high BMI):** posterior luxation zyada milta hai. Wajah: sitting mein pelvis peeche tilt hota hai, coccyx pe zyada load.
- **Patients with normal/low BMI:** spicule zyada milta hai, aur isolated hypermobility bhi. Wajah: ischial support kam, direct bone pressure.
- **Trauma history** (fall, childbirth) ka link instability (luxation/hypermobility) se dikha tha.
- Ye BMI wala relationship ka direction mujhe confident hai, par exact percentages nahi bataunga.

### Practical use
| Finding | Matlab | Management ka direction |
|---|---|---|
| Normal mobility | Instability nahi | Conservative, referred cause dhoondo |
| Hypermobility | Instability | Injection pehle, fail ho to coccygectomy discuss |
| Posterior luxation | Instability | Same |
| Spicule | Mechanical irritation | Same, spicule excision/coccygectomy |

Ye table meri summary hai, Maigne ka official algorithm nahi.

### Caveat
Maigne ke findings **asymptomatic logon mein bhi** mil sakte hain, isliye X-ray akela diagnosis nahi hai. Clinical exam aur diagnostic injection ke saath mila ke decide karo.
