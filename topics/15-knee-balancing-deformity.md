## 1. Soft-tissue balancing

- Target: **1-2 mm** balanced varus-valgus opening. Over-release may force a constrained post [[p.444]]
- **Osteophytes first**: removal alone may balance the knee

| Structure | Opens more |
|---|---|
| Anterior sMCL, pes anserinus | **Flexion** |
| Posterior sMCL, POL, posterior capsule, semimembranosus | **Extension** |
| ITB | Extension |
| Popliteus | Flexion |
| Posterolateral corner | Extension |
| LCL | Both |

> [!samajh] **Anterior = flexion, posterior = extension.**

- **Pie-crusting:** multiple stab incisions in the taut sleeve under stress; keeps a **tether**, so overshoot is less. Danger at posterolateral corner: **peroneal nerve within 1.5 cm** (flex the knee to move it away) [[p.445]]

## 2. Varus knee: PS vs CR

| | PS | CR |
|---|---|---|
| PCL | **Resect before balancing** (secondary medial stabiliser) | Stays: release may need to be **more** |
| If medial still tight | Posteromedial release, then advance LCL / CCK | **Balance PCL** (recess), anterior-lip/deep-dish, or convert to PS |

![Fig 7.48: PCL as central tether in varus: release intrasubstance, pie-crust or off tibia](figs/f7_48.jpg)

## 3. Valgus knee

:::flow
[teal] Valgus | 3-layer lateral anatomy, medial sleeve attenuated [[p.445-446]]
v
Only extension tight | ITB (pie-crust) / PLC ;; Flexion also tight | Popliteus / LCL
v
Still tight | Posterior capsule off lateral femur, then lateral gastrocnemius
v
[red] Last resort | MCL advancement or CCK (check PS post jump height)
:::

- **Peroneal palsy** after correcting severe valgus + flexion contracture: flex the knee, observe [[p.447]]

## 4. Flexion contracture and recurvatum

| | Flexion contracture | Recurvatum (< 1%) |
|---|---|---|
| Fix | Clear posterior osteophytes, strip posterior capsule, gastrocnemius origin, then **+2 mm, +2 mm distal femur (total 4 mm)** | Plan **equal gaps**: cut **less** distal femur, smaller femoral component with anterior referencing |
| Limit | **Joint line not > 4 mm up**, else midflexion instability | Neuromuscular weakness: **hinge with extension stop** |
| PCL | Does **not** contribute | - |

> [!samajh] Dono mein rule same: **gaps equal, joint line ek limit se zyada na hilao.**

:::qa Midflexion instability kyon?
Excess distal femoral resection: stable in full extension, lax in slight flexion because collaterals are relatively longer. Treat with CCK or rarely hinge [[p.447]].
:::

## 5. PCL balancing in CR (Technique 7.9)

- **Tight PCL signs:** trial poly **lifts off anteriorly in flexion** (Fig 7.52), limited flexion, rollback onto the posterior third
- **At 90 deg:** firm pressure should deflect PCL **1-2 mm**
- Steps: check **slope** > smaller femur + anterior referencing > **recess PCL** (1-2 mm subperiosteal) > convert to PS or deep-dish
- **> 75%** PCL released: use more posterior constraint [[p.449]]

## 6. Bone deficiency

![Fig 7.53: Contained (A) vs non-contained (B) defects](figs/f7_53.jpg)

| Defect | Management |
|---|---|
| < 5 mm | Cement |
| Contained | Impacted cancellous graft |
| Large non-contained | Structural graft, modular wedge/block, screws + cement |
| Revision-level | Porous metal cones, sleeves |

- Classifications: **Rand** I-III, **AORI** F1-3 / T1-3 [[p.449]]
- Technique 7.10: flatten defect, pin graft, seal junction with cement, **neutral alignment essential**, protect with stem [[p.450]]

## 7. Patellar tracking and lateral release

- **Causes of maltracking:** tibial component IR, femoral component IR / medial translation, button centred (should be **medialised**), overstuffing [[p.450]]
- **No-thumb test**, but **lower the tourniquet first**: 48% maltracked until the tourniquet was released

![Fig 7.59: Patellar button positioned medially to match native apex](figs/f7_59.jpg)

- **Lateral release:** Gerdy's tubercle to vastus lateralis fibres; outside-in lets you preserve the **superior lateral geniculate artery** [[p.452]]

![Fig 7.61: Lateral retinacular release, inside-out (A) and outside-in (B)](figs/f7_61.jpg)

> [!samajh] Order
> Tibia/femur rotation, button position, thickness check **pehle**. Lateral release **sabse last**.
