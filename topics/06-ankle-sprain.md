Reference: Kerkhoffs/van Dijk (Cochrane and ESSKA-AFAS guidelines), Campbell's, DeLee & Drez, PEACE & LOVE (Dubois & Esculier 2020). Ye working summary hai, book ka text nahi. Percentages aur timelines papers mein alag milte hain, wahan flag kiya hai. **Newer drugs wale section mein pehle fact check kar lena**, kyunki wahan evidence sabse kamzor hai aur 2026 ke updates mujhe pakke nahi pata.

## 1. Basics
- ~85% ankle sprains **lateral** hote hain. Mechanism: **inversion + plantarflexion**.
- **Sequence of injury:** ATFL (sabse pehle, aur sabse zyada) → CFL → PTFL (rare, dislocation level).
- **Syndesmotic ("high ankle")** sprain: dorsiflexion + external rotation. Alag entity hai, section 8.
- **Medial (deltoid)** sprain: eversion injury, isolated rare hai, aksar fracture ke saath.
- Sports mein sabse common injury hai. **Recurrence aur chronic instability** is topic ka asli problem hai, acute phase nahi.

## 2. Pehli visit: red flags (sprain bolne se pehle ye rule out karo)

**Ottawa Ankle Rules (X-ray kab):**
- **Malleolar zone pain** + koi ek:
  - Posterior edge/tip of lateral ya medial malleolus pe bone tenderness (distal 6 cm)
  - **4 steps weight bear nahi kar paya** (injury ke time aur ED/OPD mein dono)
- **Midfoot zone pain** + koi ek:
  - **5th metatarsal base** tenderness
  - **Navicular** tenderness
- Sensitivity ~100% ke kareeb, isliye negative ho to X-ray skip karna safe hai. Par rules **children (<~5-6 yrs), intoxicated, neuropathy, multiple injuries** mein reliable nahi.

**Red flags / sprain nahi hai, ya zyada serious hai:**
| Red flag | Soch |
|---|---|
| Ottawa positive | Malleolar/5th MT/navicular fracture |
| **Proximal fibula tenderness** | **Maisonneuve** injury (syndesmosis + fibula). Poori leg X-ray lo |
| Squeeze/ER test positive, high ankle pain | Syndesmotic injury |
| Lateral process of talus tenderness (snowboarder) | Talus fracture, X-ray mein miss hota hai, CT |
| Anterior process of calcaneus tenderness | Avulsion fracture |
| Tense swelling, severe pain, pain on passive toe stretch | **Compartment syndrome** (rare) |
| Cold/pale foot, pulse absent | Vascular injury / dislocation |
| Numbness, foot drop | Peroneal/tibial nerve injury, **traction neuropraxia** |
| Open wound / deformity | Fracture-dislocation |
| Fever, red, hot, no clear trauma | Septic arthritis (sprain nahi) |
| Bilateral/atraumatic recurrent swelling | Inflammatory ya systemic cause |

## 3. Assessment

**Timing ka rule (high yield):** Acute phase mein pain, swelling aur guarding ki wajah se **drawer/talar tilt unreliable** hote hain. **Delayed physical exam 4-5 din baad** (van Dijk) zyada sensitive aur specific hai. Isliye pehli visit pe fracture rule out karo, aur reassess 5 din baad karo.

**Delayed exam ke findings:**
- **Hematoma** lateral malleolus ke neeche (ATFL/CFL tear ka sign)
- **Pain on palpation of ATFL** (anterior to lateral malleolus)
- **Anterior drawer** (talus anterior translation, side to side compare). Positive = ATFL.
- **Talar tilt (inversion stress)** = CFL (aur ATFL).
- **Squeeze test, external rotation test, cotton test** = syndesmosis.

Syndesmosis aur peroneal tendons (subluxation, tear) ko bhi har baar check karo.

## 4. Grading (practical, kyunki recovery time isi se predict hota hai)
| Grade | Injury | Findings | Typical outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | ATFL stretch | Mild swelling, weight bear ho jaata hai, stable | Jaldi recover |
| II | Partial ATFL ± CFL | Moderate swelling, ecchymosis, mild laxity | Medium |
| III | Complete ATFL + CFL | Severe, unable to bear weight, laxity | Lamba |

> [!verify] Flag: grading systems alag hain, aur clinical grade aur outcome ka correlation weak hai.

## 5. Acute management: PEACE & LOVE (RICE/POLICE ki jagah)
Naya concept: **POLICE ke baad PEACE & LOVE** aaya, kyunki ice aur NSAIDs ka healing pe asar question mein hai.

**PEACE (first days):**
- **P**rotect: 1-3 din, zyada pain wale movement avoid karo.
- **E**levate.
- **A**void anti-inflammatories: NSAIDs ko early healing ka nuksan ho sakta hai, aisi theory hai. **Flag:** isme debate hai, short course NSAID practice mein bahut use hota hai aur pain control mein theek hai. Ice bhi unke hisaab se avoid, par ye consensus nahi hai.
- **C**ompress: bandage/tape.
- **E**ducate: passive modalities nahi, **active recovery** pe zor.

**LOVE (baad mein):**
- **L**oad: pain allow kare utna weight bearing **jaldi** shuru.
- **O**ptimism: recovery ke baare mein positive counselling.
- **V**ascularisation: pain-free cardio.
- **E**xercise: mobility, strength, balance/proprioception.

**Immobilisation:** Evidence (Kerkhoffs, Cochrane) kehta hai ki **functional treatment** (semi-rigid lace-up brace ya taping, early mobilisation) **cast se behtar ya barabar hai.** Cast ya boot sirf **short course (~5-10 din tak)** severe pain mein, nahi to stiffness, atrophy, aur recovery lamba hoti hai. Grade III mein bhi functional treatment primary hai.

**Surgery acute mein:** routine nahi. Functional treatment pe outcome aksar surgery jaisa hai. Sirf selected elite athletes mein discuss hota hai.

## 6. Pharma: kya kaam karta hai, naye agents kya hain

| Agent | Kaam kya | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Paracetamol** | Pain relief | Safe first-line |
| **Topical NSAID (diclofenac gel)** | Local pain relief, GI side effect kam | **Reasonably good** evidence for acute sprain pain |
| **Oral NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen, etoricoxib)** | Pain, swelling | Short-term pain mein useful. Healing pe asar ka debate (PEACE). Short course rakho |
| **Topical lidocaine/ketorolac patch** | Local pain | Limited, par safe |
| **Enzyme combos (serratiopeptidase, trypsin-chymotrypsin, bromelain)** | Swelling | **Bharat mein bahut use hote hain, evidence weak/inconsistent.** Isse kaam chalta hai, par main claim nahi karunga ki proven hai |
| **Periarticular hyaluronic acid injection** | Pain, function, recovery | **Kuch RCTs (Petrella et al.) mein faster return to sport**, par studies chhoti, aur practice mein standard nahi |
| **PRP** | Healing | **Acute sprain mein evidence mixed/negative.** Ek ED RCT mein koi fayda nahi mila. Chronic instability mein experimental |
| **Corticosteroid injection** | Pain | **Nahi dena.** Ligament weaken karta hai |
| **Stem cell/prolotherapy** | Ligament healing | Experimental, routine mat karo |

**Direct baat:** Mujhe pakka nahi pata ki 2026 mein koi **naya approved drug** hai jo ankle sprain ki healing speed badhata ho. Jo mujhe pata hai (HA, PRP) sab limited evidence wale hain. Agar tu kisi specific "naye agent" ke baare mein soch raha hai, bata, main search karke verify kar deta hoon, kyunki ye area recency-sensitive hai.

## 7. Recovery pattern (is topic ka core)

**Typical timeline (rough, flag):**
| Grade | Pain-free daily activity | Return to sport |
|---|---|---|
| I | ~1-2 weeks | ~2-3 weeks |
| II | ~3-6 weeks | ~4-8 weeks |
| III | ~6-12 weeks | ~3-4+ months |
| High ankle (syndesmotic) | **~2x lamba**, weeks to months | Aksar 2-3 months+ |

**Phases:**
1. **Acute (0-3 days):** protect, compress, elevate, controlled pain control.
2. **Subacute (days 3 se ~2 weeks):** weight bearing as tolerated, ROM (**ankle alphabet**, dorsiflexion), isometrics.
3. **Strengthening (~2-6 weeks):** peroneal strengthening (eversion bands), calf raises, gait training.
4. **Proprioception/balance (parallel):** single-leg stand, wobble board, eyes closed. **Ye sabse important hai recurrence rokne ke liye.**
5. **Return to sport (~4-8+ weeks):** hopping, cutting, agility, sport-specific drills, brace/taping.

**Hidden problem (counsel karo):**
- Bahut log "theek" ho jaate hain, par **~30-40% mein persistent symptoms** (pain, swelling, giving way) 1 saal baad bhi rehte hain (Doherty et al. type data, flag).
- Kuch literature mein **chronic ankle instability ~20-40%** acute sprain ke baad.
- **Recurrence** high hai pehle 1 saal mein.

## 8. High ankle sprain (syndesmosis) ka short, alag section
- Mechanism: **dorsiflexion + external rotation** (football, skiing).
- Exam: **squeeze test, external rotation stress, cotton test**, pain anterior syndesmosis.
- Imaging: weight bearing X-ray (widening), **MRI** (grading), **CT** doubt mein.
- **Stable (no diastasis):** boot/protected weight bearing ~2-6 weeks, functional rehab. Recovery lamba hai.
- **Unstable (diastasis, latent diastasis):** **surgery**, suture-button (TightRope) ya screw fixation.
- Isko normal sprain ki tarah treat karoge to patient 6 hafte baad bhi pain bol kar wapas aayega.

## 9. 6 hafte baad bhi pain? Workup (missed injury checklist)
Agar ~6 weeks baad **pain, swelling, ya giving way** hai to ye dhoondo:
- **Osteochondral lesion of talus (OLT)** (sabse commonly missed, MRI)
- **Syndesmotic injury** (missed)
- **Peroneal tendon tear/subluxation**
- **Lateral process of talus ya anterior process of calcaneus fracture**
- **5th MT base fracture (Jones/avulsion)**
- **Sinus tarsi syndrome / subtalar instability**
- **Anterolateral impingement** (soft tissue, scar)
- **CRPS** (disproportionate pain)
- **Tarsal tunnel / peroneal nerve neuropraxia**
- **Medial ligament injury**

Plan: weight bearing X-ray + **MRI**, CT jahan bone suspect ho.

## 10. Chronic ankle instability (CAI)

**Types:**
- **Functional instability:** giving way, apprehension, proprioception aur strength deficit. Ligament structurally theek ho sakta hai.
- **Mechanical instability:** ATFL/CFL attenuated/torn, **anterior drawer/talar tilt positive.**
- Dono saath bhi ho sakte hain.

**Treatment:**
1. **Conservative (hamesha pehle, ~3-6 months):**
   - **Structured neuromuscular + proprioceptive rehab** (supervised).
   - Peroneal strengthening, balance training.
   - **Brace/taping** sports mein.
   - Evidence: rehab hi bahut cases mein functional improvement deta hai.
2. **Surgery (jab conservative fail, recurrent giving way):**
   - **Modified Broström-Gould** (ATFL ± CFL anatomic repair + inferior extensor retinaculum augmentation). **Gold standard.** Results achhe (~85-90% good).
   - **Arthroscopic Broström** (kam invasive, popular ho raha hai).
   - **Augmentation/reconstruction (allograft/autograft tendon, InternalBrace, suture tape)** jab **tissue poor, high BMI, generalised laxity, revision, high-demand athlete.**
   - **Hindfoot varus** hai to **calcaneal osteotomy** (Dwyer) saath mein, warna repair fail ho sakta hai.
   - **Associated pathology** (OLT, peroneal tear, impingement) ko scope mein ek saath treat karo.
3. **Post-op:** boot ~2-3 weeks (protected), phir progressive rehab, **return to sport ~3-4 months**, flag.

## 11. Patient ko kya bolna hai (OPD script)
- "Pehle X-ray ki zaroorat hai ya nahi ye rule se decide kiya, abhi fracture nahi lag raha."
- "Cast ki zaroorat nahi, **brace aur early walking** se jaldi theek hoge."
- "**Dard theek hone ka matlab ligament theek hona nahi hai.** Balance exercises zaroor karo, warna dobara moch aayegi."
- "Zyada tar 2-6 hafte mein normal activity, sport mein 4-8 hafte, high ankle ya severe grade mein zyada time."
- "Agar **6 hafte baad bhi dard ya giving way** hai to wapas aana, kuch aur chhoota hua ho sakta hai."

## 12. Quick OPD/OT rules {revise}
1. **Ottawa rules pehle**, X-ray decide karo.
2. **Proximal fibula, syndesmosis, talus process, 5th MT** miss mat karo.
3. Acute exam unreliable, **5 din baad dobara exam.**
4. **Functional treatment (brace + early load) > cast.**
5. **Steroid injection nahi.**
6. **Proprioceptive rehab** recurrence rokta hai, ye skip mat karo.
7. **6 weeks persistent pain = MRI, OLT/syndesmosis/peroneal dhoondo.**
8. Chronic instability mein **rehab pehle, surgery baad mein**, Broström-Gould standard.

## Lateral ligament complex: ATFL, CFL, PTFL

Haan, teeno **lateral ligament complex** ka hi part hain, alag scene nahi hai. Bas teeno ek saath nahi phat'te, ek **sequence** mein phat'te hain, aur har ek ka role alag hai.

### Teeno ki anatomy aur kaam

| Ligament | Kahan se kahan | Kaam | Kab test hota hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ATFL** | Fibula (lateral malleolus) anterior border → talus neck | **Plantarflexion mein** inversion rokta hai, talus ko aage khisakne se rokta hai | **Anterior drawer** (plantarflexed ankle) |
| **CFL** | Fibula tip → calcaneus (peroneal tendons ke neeche se) | **Dorsiflexion/neutral mein** inversion rokta hai, **ankle + subtalar dono** cross karta hai | **Talar tilt** (neutral/dorsiflexion) |
| **PTFL** | Fibula posterior → talus posterior (posterior process) | Extreme dorsiflexion mein stabiliser, talus ko peeche/rotation se rokta hai | Clinically alag test nahi |

### Injury sequence kyun yehi hai
- **Inversion + plantarflexion** mein ATFL **sabse pehle taut** hota hai, to wahi sabse pehle phat'ta hai. Isliye **isolated ATFL tear sabse common** hai (~70% ke aas-paas, flag).
- Force aur badhe to **CFL** phat'ta hai. **ATFL + CFL** combo Grade III kehlata hai.
- **PTFL** bahut strong hai aur tabhi phat'ta hai jab **ankle dislocate** ho ya bahut severe injury ho. Isolated PTFL tear **rare** hai.

### Kuch aur ligaments jinhe log lateral sprain mein bhool jaate hain
- **Lateral talocalcaneal ligament, cervical ligament, interosseous talocalcaneal ligament:** subtalar stability dete hain. Inka damage **subtalar instability** ya sinus tarsi pain ka reason ban sakta hai.
- **Inferior extensor retinaculum:** Broström-Gould mein augmentation ke liye use hota hai.
- **Peroneal tendons (brevis, longus):** dynamic stabiliser, inversion rokte hain. Inka tear ya subluxation bhi sprain jaisa dikhta hai.

### Isse alag: kya "lateral sprain" ka part nahi hai
- **Syndesmosis (AITFL, PITFL, interosseous):** tibia-fibula ke beech. **High ankle sprain** alag entity hai.
- **Deltoid ligament:** medial side.

### Practical use
- **Anterior drawer positive** = ATFL.
- **Talar tilt positive** = CFL (aksar ATFL ke saath).
- **Dono positive** = ATFL + CFL, Grade III, mechanical instability ka chance zyada.
- Chronic instability surgery (Broström) mein **ATFL aur CFL** repair hota hai. PTFL ko touch nahi karte.

**Ek line mein:** ATFL (aage), CFL (beech/neeche), PTFL (peeche) = lateral ligament complex ke teen hisse. Sprain mein ATFL almost hamesha, CFL kabhi kabhi, PTFL bahut rare.

## Ankle stability tests: step by step

Ye tests **delayed exam (4-5 din baad)** mein zyada reliable hain. Acute mein pain aur guarding se false negative aata hai. Hamesha **dono side compare** karo, normal side pehle.

### 1. Anterior drawer test (ATFL)

**Position:**
- Patient baitha ho ya supine, knee flexed (gastrocnemius relax rahe), foot ledge se bahar.
- Ankle **10-20° plantarflexion** mein. Neutral mein CFL aur capsule rok dete hain, isliye plantarflexion mein ATFL isolate hota hai.

**Kaise karna hai:**
1. Ek haath se **distal tibia ke aage** pakdo aur stabilise karo (peeche ki taraf halka push).
2. Doosre haath se **heel (calcaneus) ko cup** karo.
3. Heel ko **aage ki taraf kheencho**, tibia ko peeche rakhte hue. Talus tibia ke neeche se aage khisakta hai.

**Kya dekhna hai:**
- **Anterior translation**: normal side se zyada khisakna (ek approx figure **>3 mm side to side difference ya >10 mm absolute** hai, flag).
- **Dimple/suction sign**: lateral malleolus ke aage skin mein gadda (talus aage khisakne se). ATFL tear ka sign.
- **Soft/mushy end-point** (normal mein firm).

**Positive = ATFL deficient.**

### 2. Talar tilt test (CFL, aur ATFL)

**Position:**
- Same, patient relaxed, ankle **neutral (0°)** mein. Neutral mein CFL taut hota hai, isliye wo isolate hota hai.

**Kaise karna hai:**
1. Ek haath se **distal tibia/fibula ke upar** pakdo (leg stabilise).
2. Doosre haath se **heel pakdo** aur **inversion force** lagao (talus ko tibia ke andar tilt karo).
3. Talus **tibial plafond se kitna khulta hai**, wo feel karo.

**Kya dekhna hai:**
- **Side to side difference** (flag: **>5-10° difference** ya **>10° absolute** ko abnormal maante hain, authors mein alag).
- Soft end-point.

**Interpretation:**
- Neutral mein tilt **zyada** = **CFL** involved.
- Plantarflexion mein bhi tilt kar sakte ho, wo **ATFL** ka contribution dikhata hai.
- Dono positive = ATFL + CFL, Grade III.

### 3. Squeeze test (syndesmosis)

**Position:** patient baitha ya supine, leg relaxed.

**Kaise karna hai:**
1. Calf ke **mid-level pe** (knee se ~ 10-15 cm neeche) tibia aur fibula ko **ek saath pakdo**.
2. **Squeeze karo**, tibia-fibula ko ek dusre ki taraf dabao.
3. Pain **neeche, ankle ke upar (syndesmosis area) mein** feel ho to positive.

**Isse kya hota hai:** Fibula tibia ki taraf dabti hai, to syndesmosis pe stress aata hai (injured ho to pain).

**Positive = syndesmotic injury** (pain anterior syndesmosis ya distal area mein, proximal pain nahi. Proximal fibula pain Maisonneuve suspect karao).

**Note:** Squeeze test aur external rotation test **specificity** achhi deti hain par **sensitivity** limited. Ek negative test se rule out mat karo.

### 4. External rotation test (Kleiger test)

**Position:** patient baitha, knee **90° flexed**, leg hanging, ankle **neutral dorsiflexion (90°)**.

**Kaise karna hai:**
1. Ek haath se **leg ko stable pakdo** (tibia ke upar).
2. Doosre haath se **foot ko pakdo** (heel aur forefoot).
3. **Foot ko externally rotate karo** (talus fibula ko bahar dhakelta hai).

**Kya dekhna hai:**
- **Anterior syndesmosis pe pain** (AITFL), ya medial side pe pain (deltoid).
- Gap/widening feel ho to instability.

**Positive = syndesmosis (ya deltoid) injury.**

### 5. Cotton test (syndesmosis instability)

**Position:** ankle neutral, patient relaxed.

**Kaise karna hai:**
1. Ek haath se **tibia ko stabilise** karo.
2. Doosre haath se **heel pakdo**, aur **talus ko lateral direction mein** (bahar ki taraf) kheencho. Isse talus fibula ko dhakelta hai.
3. Dekhna hai **fibula tibia se kitna alag hota hai**.

**Positive:** fibula ka **excess lateral translation** (>3-4 mm, flag). Ya fluoroscopy mein syndesmotic widening.

**Isse kya pata chalta hai:** syndesmosis **unstable** hai ya nahi (stable vs unstable decision, jo surgery decide karta hai).

**Best practice:** Cotton test ko **fluoroscopy ke saath** (stress view) karna zyada accurate hai, bina fluoro ke subjective hota hai.

### Quick summary table

| Test | Ankle position | Force | Kya check karta hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anterior drawer** | Plantarflexed 10-20° | Heel aage kheencho | **ATFL** |
| **Talar tilt** | Neutral | Inversion | **CFL** (± ATFL) |
| **Squeeze** | Neutral | Mid-calf pe tibia-fibula squeeze | **Syndesmosis** |
| **External rotation (Kleiger)** | 90° dorsiflexion, knee 90° | Foot external rotate | **Syndesmosis/deltoid** |
| **Cotton** | Neutral | Talus lateral kheencho | **Syndesmotic instability** |

### Practical tips
1. **Normal side pehle**, patient ko baseline pata chale (aur tujhe).
2. **Patient relaxed ho**, guarding hai to test unreliable.
3. **Side to side compare** karo, absolute numbers se zyada reliable.
4. **Acute mein pain hota hai**, to 4-5 din baad dobara karo.
5. **Positive test + doubt** hai to **stress X-ray/MRI** lo.

**Caveat:** Cutoff numbers (mm, degrees) alag books aur papers mein alag hain, to viva mein "side to side difference" bolna safe hai, fixed number quote karna risky.

## Supplements: Tendocare, T-Heal, Macliga (role in sprain)

Short answer: **ankle sprain mein inka koi proven role nahi hai.** Ye supplements hain, drugs nahi, aur sprain pe inke koi solid RCT mujhe nahi pata.

### Kya hai inme (search se verify kiya)
- **Tendocare Forte (Pharmed):** bilayered tablet jisme L-arginine 500 mg (SR), chondroitin sulfate 200 mg, collagen peptide type I 40 mg, sodium hyaluronate 30 mg aur vitamin C hain. Company ise advancing aur traumatic tendinopathy ke liye promote karti hai.
- **T-Heal Forte:** nutraceutical tablet, collagen peptides type I aur chondroitin sulfate jaise ingredients ke saath, aur label pe "not for medicinal use" likha hai. Alag platforms pe T-Heal capsule aur tablet ki listing alag dikhti hai, to apni strip ka label dekh lena.
- **Macliga:** mujhe iski composition search mein mili hi nahi. Strip ka composition bhej do, phir bata dunga.

### Evidence kyun weak hai
1. **Dose ka gap:** jin studies mein collagen peptides ka fayda dikha, unme roz ke **gram level** (~5-15 g) dose the (as far as I remember). Tendocare mein collagen sirf **40 mg** hai. Ye ~100x kam hai.
2. **Jo studies hain wo sprain pe nahi hain.** Collagen peptides pe chronic ankle instability aur Achilles tendinopathy mein chhoti studies hain (Dressler 2018, Praet 2019, memory se, verify kar lena). Wo chronic cases hain aur aksar industry-funded. Acute sprain ki healing speed pe data nahi hai.
3. **Chondroitin aur oral hyaluronate** ka data osteoarthritis ke liye hai, ligament healing ke liye nahi. Oral HA ka sprain mein role nahi hai (jo HA ke fayde ke trials hain wo periarticular injection ke hain).
4. **L-arginine aur vitamin C:** wound healing ki theoretical logic hai, ankle sprain mein koi proof nahi.

### Practical
- Acute sprain mein recovery **functional brace + early loading + proprioceptive rehab** se hoti hai. Koi tablet iski jagah nahi le sakti.
- Agar patient ko dena hi hai to harmless hai, par ~₹35/tablet ka cost hai (roughly ₹500+ per strip, dinon tak). Patient ko bolo ki ye **optional hai, recovery fast karne ka claim proven nahi.**
- **Chronic instability ya tendinopathy** mein collagen supplement ka argument thoda behtar hai, par wahan bhi proper dose (grams) aur rehab ke saath.
- Jo cheez swelling/pain mein practice mein chalti hai wo enzyme combos hain (bromelain + trypsin + rutoside, jaise A-Heal type). Unka evidence bhi weak/inconsistent hai.
- **Pharma rep claims** ko is nazar se dekho: kaunsa RCT, kitne patients, kaunsi condition, kaunsa dose, aur kisne fund kiya. Sprain pe jawab aksar "koi nahi" hota hai.

Macliga ka composition bhej do to uska bhi alag se dekh leta hoon.

### Kya fark padta hai L-arginine se?
Company ka logic hai ki L-arginine nitric oxide se blood supply aur collagen synthesis badhata hai. Ye theory hai. Tendon ya ligament healing mein oral L-arginine ka koi solid human trial mujhe nahi pata. To "Forte" lene ka evidence-based reason nahi hai.

### Kab dena make sense hai (honest version)
**Thoda argument hai:**
- **Chronic tendinopathy** (tennis elbow, Achilles, rotator cuff tendinopathy, patellar) mein, **eccentric/loading rehab ke saath adjunct** ki tarah.
- **Chronic ankle instability** mein rehab ke saath (collagen peptides pe is condition mein chhoti study hai).
- **Post-op tendon/ligament repair rehab** mein, patient chahe aur afford kar sake.

**Nahi dena / koi fayda nahi:**
- **Acute ankle sprain**, fracture ya acute muscle strain. Recovery rehab se hoti hai.
- Jab patient ki rehab compliance kharab ho aur wo "dawai se theek ho jaunga" soch raha ho.

### Counselling ke liye must-know
1. **Dose bahut kam hai.** Collagen 40 mg hai, jabki jin studies mein fayda dikha unme gram level dose the (memory se, verify kar lena). To trial evidence in tablets pe seedha lagu nahi hota.
2. **Cost:** ~₹26-35 per tablet. Agar TDS dein to ~₹80-100/day, matlab mahine ka ₹2400-3000. Patient ko ye pehle bata do.
3. **Non-vegetarian source** (animal-derived) hai, vegetarian patient ko pehle puchh lo.
4. **Expectation:** "support" wali cheez hai, healing speed badhane ka proof nahi.
5. Chondroitin ke saath warfarin/anticoagulant mein caution, aur L-arginine low BP wale mein dhyan se.
