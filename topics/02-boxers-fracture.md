## Big picture

Fracture of the **neck of the 5th metacarpal**, usually from punching a hard surface. The head tilts toward the palm so the apex of the break points to the back of the hand.

> [!samajh] Haddi ka sirra (apex) peeche ki taraf nikalta hai, head palm ki taraf jhuk jaata hai. Isliye "dorsal angulation / apex dorsal". Interossei muscles distal tukde ko flexion mein kheechti hain, wahi deformity banati hain.

## Look for these first {revise}

- **Open wound over the knuckle = fight bite** until proven otherwise. Human mouth bacteria. Needs washout and antibiotics, not just a cast.
- **Rotation**: ask the patient to make a fist. Fingers must point toward the **scaphoid tubercle**. Overlap or scissoring means rotational malalignment.
- Loss of the knuckle contour, a pseudo-claw look (MCP hyperextended, IP flexed) when the patient tries to extend.
- Tenderness at the base, other metacarpals, wrist: check the whole hand.

## X-ray

- PA, lateral and oblique of the hand.
- Measure the angle on the **true lateral**. The 4th and 5th are mobile at the CMC joint, so they tolerate more angulation than the 2nd and 3rd.

## What is acceptable? {revise}

| Item | Accepted for non-operative |
|---|---|
| Angulation (5th neck) | up to roughly 40 to 70 degrees |
| Rotation | **none** |
| Shortening | small, about 5 mm or less |
| Skin | closed |

> [!verify] Angulation limits differ between books (30, 40, 50, up to 70 degrees). Confirm your textbook's numbers before quoting them in an exam. Rotation is the non-negotiable.

> [!flag] Clinical rotation matters more than the angle on the X-ray.

:::flow
[teal] Closed, no rotation, acceptable angle
v
Functional treatment: buddy tape + early motion
v
[amber] Rotation / open / multiple MC / unacceptable angle / failed
v
Reduce, then pin or plate
:::

## Treatment options

### Functional treatment (for most)
- Short ulnar-gutter splint or buddy strapping for comfort, then **early movement**.
- Evidence supports it: results are similar to casting, with faster return of movement and function. The cosmetic bump of the knuckle may remain.
- **IP joints stay free**, so fingers do not stiffen.

### Reduction (when needed)
- Jahss manoeuvre: flex MCP and PIP to 90 degrees, push **up** along the proximal phalanx so it pushes the head up. Ulnar nerve block or haematoma block for anaesthesia.
- Immobilise in the **intrinsic plus position** (MCP flexed about 70 to 90 degrees, IPs straight). That tightens the collateral ligaments and keeps them from shortening.

> [!verify] How many weeks your book recommends for cast/splint. Usually 3 to 4.

### Surgery
- Retrograde intramedullary pins (bouquet) or transverse **intermetacarpal pinning** to the 4th metacarpal (3 weeks).
- Plate and screws for comminuted, multiple or intra-articular patterns.

## Complications

- Malunion (knuckle flattening, pseudo-claw) and rotational deformity.
- MCP stiffness if immobilised in extension for too long.
- Infection if a fight bite is missed.

## Viva {viva}

:::qa Why does the apex point dorsally?
Interossei pull the distal fragment into flexion, so the head tilts volar and the apex goes dorsal.
:::

:::qa What matters more than angulation?
Rotation, found by looking at fingers when a fist is made.
:::

:::qa Why immobilise in intrinsic plus?
Tightens the MCP collaterals and prevents contracture.
:::

:::qa What do you suspect with a small wound over the knuckle?
A fight bite. Wash out and give antibiotics.
:::
