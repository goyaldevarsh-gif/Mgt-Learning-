Reference: Green's Operative Hand Surgery, Rockwood & Green's Fractures in Adults, Campbell's (hand chapters). Ye book ka copy nahi, working summary hai. Angulation thresholds alag books mein thode alag milte hain, wo neeche flag kiya hai.

## 1. Basics (samajh lo pehle)
- **Mechanism:** clenched fist se axial load (mukka marna, deewar/aadmi ko). Usually young male.
- **Naming trivia:** classically "boxer's fracture" index/middle MC ka hota hai (trained boxers). 5th MC neck ka sahi naam "street fighter's fracture" hai, par clinically sab "boxer's" bolte hain.
- **Deformity kyun:** distal fragment **flexion + apex dorsal angulation** mein jaata hai kyunki interossei aur volar plate pull karte hain. Volar cortex aksar **comminuted** hota hai, isliye reduction hold nahi hota.
- **Knuckle flat** dikhta hai aur MC head palm mein chubhta hai.

## 2. OPD assessment (yahan decision banta hai)
1. **Wound check:** knuckle pe koi cut/tooth mark = **FIGHT BITE** jab tak sabit na ho. Ye alag emergency hai (neeche section 7).
2. **ROTATION check (sabse important):**
   - Fingers flex karke dekho, sabhi fingers **scaphoid tubercle** ki taraf point karne chahiye.
   - **Scissoring/overlap** = rotational malalignment = surgery ki taraf.
   - Nail plates ka alignment bhi dekho.
3. **Pseudoclawing:** MCP hyperextension, PIP flexion, extensor lag. Zyada angulation ka sign.
4. Neurovascular status, ulnar nerve sensation.
5. **X-ray:** AP, lateral, oblique. Lateral mein 4th/5th MC overlap karte hain, isliye **30° semi-supinated lateral** lo. Angle = metacarpal shaft axis aur head/neck axis ke beech.

## 3. Kitna angulation accept hai?
Reason: 4th/5th CMC joints mobile hain (5th ~25-30°, 4th ~15°). Isliye ulnar side zyada deformity compensate kar leti hai.

| Metacarpal | Neck angulation tolerance (classic teaching) |
|---|---|
| Index | ~10° |
| Middle | ~20° |
| Ring | ~30° |
| **Little** | **~40° (classic), many accept up to 50-70°** |

- **Flag:** 40° classic Rockwood/Green teaching hai. Newer literature aur RCTs mein 60-70° tak ke saath bhi function achha aata hai. Isliye practically: **cosmetic aur function mein jo bachega wahi decide karta hai.**
- **Shortening:** ≤ 5 mm accept. Har ~2 mm shortening = ~7° extensor lag (approx).
- **Rotation:** **zero tolerance.** Clinical scissoring chahe angle kuch bhi ho.

## 4. Conservative: kab aur kaise

**Kab (ideal candidate):**
- Closed, isolated, angulation acceptable range mein
- Rotation normal, shortening ≤ 5 mm
- Pseudoclawing nahi ya mild
- Patient ko cosmetic knuckle loss se issue nahi

**Kaise (evidence-based, yaad rakho):**
- Reduction **zaroori nahi** agar angulation acceptable hai.
- **Functional treatment** (compression bandage/soft wrap ya buddy strapping, early movement) cast jaisa hi result deta hai aur **early ROM, early return to work**. Cochrane review aur RCTs (Statius Muller et al.) yahi dikhate hain. Isliye ye ek safe, evidence-supported option hai, aur cast se kam nahi.

> [!verify] Cochrane ne khud kaha hai ki trials chhote hain aur evidence quality low hai, to ye firm recommendation nahi hai. Practice mein ulnar gutter splint/cast abhi bhi bahut jagah diya jaata hai.
- Cast chahiye ho (pain, compliance) to: **ulnar gutter splint/cast**, MCP ~70-90° flexion, IPJ free. 3-4 weeks. **IP joints mat bandhna** (stiffness).
- Pain control, elevation, early finger motion.
- Follow-up X-ray ~1 week (displacement check), phir 3-4 weeks.
- Union ~4-6 weeks, sports/contact ~6-8 weeks.

**Reduction karna ho to (Jahss manoeuvre):**
- Haematoma/ulnar nerve block.
- MCP aur PIP dono **90°** flex karo. Isse collateral ligaments tight hote hain aur proximal phalanx ek "handle" ban jaata hai.
- Proximal phalanx ke through **dorsal push** metacarpal head pe, aur dorsal shaft pe counter-pressure.
- **Limitation:** volar comminution ki wajah se reduction aksar hold nahi hota. To ye often sirf temporary hota hai.

## 5. Operative indications (yahi sabse zaroori)
Surgery karo agar:
- **Rotational malalignment/scissoring** (non-negotiable)
- **Angulation bahut zyada** (> ~60-70°) ya patient ko functional/cosmetic issue (pseudoclawing)
- **Shortening > 5 mm**
- **Open fracture** (fight bite alag)
- **Multiple metacarpal fractures** (instability)
- **Intra-articular head involvement** ya displaced
- **Failed closed reduction/loss of reduction**
- **Special patient demands:** musician, surgeon, manual labourer needing early return, athlete
- Associated injuries (tendon, NV)

Thumb rule: **Rotation galat = operate. Angle sirf bada ho to patient aur function dekhke.**

## 6. Operative techniques (kya, kab, kaise)

**A. Closed reduction + percutaneous K-wire (CRPP), sabse common**
- Fluoroscopy/image intensifier.
- **Transverse (intermetacarpal) pinning:** 2 K-wires (1.2-1.6 mm) 5th MC se 4th MC mein across the fracture, proximal aur distal. Simple, quick. Risk: 4th MC ko bhi tether karta hai.
- **Crossed K-wires (retrograde):** wires head ke dono sides se enter karke opposite cortex pakadte hain. MCP flexion mein dalo taaki collateral ligament mein na fanse.
- Wires 3-4 weeks pe nikalte hain (buried ya exposed). Pin tract care.

**B. Intramedullary pinning, Foucher "Bouquet" technique**
- Metacarpal base ke paas **dorsal-ulnar cortex window** se 2-3 pre-bent K-wires (1.6-2.0 mm) andar daalo, fracture cross karke head tak. Wires head mein **fan out** hote hain, jisse stability aur rotation control milta hai.
- Pros: minimally invasive, joint spare, early ROM, achhi aesthetic.
- Cons: comminuted ya short head fragment mein weak.

**C. Retrograde/antegrade intramedullary screw (headless compression screw)**
- Good for **transverse/short oblique** fractures with adequate isthmus. Early mobilisation.
- Retrograde: MCP head ke dorsal cartilage se entry, to joint damage/hardware prominence ka risk. Antegrade se ye avoid hota hai (base se entry).
- Needs proper size selection aur fluoroscopy.

**D. ORIF with plate (2.0-2.4 mm mini plate / locking T- or condylar plate)**
- **Indications:** comminuted, multiple MC fractures, intra-articular, segmental bone loss, or need for rigid fixation + immediate motion.
- Dorsal approach: longitudinal incision, extensor tendon retract/split, dorsal plate (tension side). Soft tissue stripping zyada, to **tendon adhesion aur extensor lag** ka risk.
- Plate removal kabhi kabhi zaroori.

**E. Tension band/interosseous wiring**
- Comminuted ya bone-poor cases mein alternative. Less commonly used.

**Choice ka guide:**
| Situation | Technique |
|---|---|
| Simple transverse/short oblique, closed | CRPP ya bouquet |
| Comminuted, multiple MC, intra-articular | Plate (ORIF) |
| Adequate isthmus, early ROM needed | IM headless screw |
| Contaminated/open | Debridement + K-wires (plate avoid) |

## 7. Fight bite (miss mat karna)
- Knuckle pe small cut/tooth mark after punching teeth = human bite, high infection risk.
- **Management:** X-ray (tooth fragment, fracture), antibiotics (**amoxicillin-clavulanate**; severe case IV), **surgical washout/debridement** (joint + extensor), delayed closure. Primary closure nahi.
- Tendon/joint capsule involvement check karo. Late presentation = septic arthritis/osteomyelitis risk.

## 8. Post-op & rehab (tera interest area)
- **Early active ROM** from day 1-3 (stable fixation ho to). Hand therapist ke saath.
- K-wires: splint 2-3 weeks, wires 3-4 weeks pe nikalo, phir mobilisation.
- Plate/screw: immediate ROM.
- Strengthening/grip 6 weeks ke baad, **contact sports ~8-12 weeks**.
- Stiffness avoid karne ke liye **IP joints ko free rakho**.

## 9. Complications
- Malunion (rotational/angular), **extensor lag**, loss of knuckle contour
- **Stiffness** (MCP/PIP), adhesions
- Pin tract infection, hardware prominence
- Nonunion (rare)
- CRPS (rare)
- Infection (fight bite miss hone par)

## 10. 5 OPD/OT rules
1. Knuckle pe cut = fight bite, pehle usse nipto.
2. **Rotation check pehle**, angle baad mein.
3. Angulation 40-70° tak aksar conservative bhi theek, patient aur function pe depend.
4. Functional treatment (bandage + early ROM) cast se kam nahi.
5. Operate karo to simple fracture mein **CRPP/bouquet**, comminuted ya multiple mein **plate**.

## Samajh: deformity aur reduction slip kyun hota hai

Bilkul simple karte hain, 3 cheezein hain: **force, pull, aur comminution.**

**1. Pull kaun kar raha hai (deformity ka reason)**
Metacarpal neck fracture hone ke baad head (distal fragment) ke paas koi bone support nahi bachta. Ab muscles apna kaam karte hain:
- **Interossei** (aur lumbricals) MC head ke **volar** side se guzarte hain. Ye distal fragment ko **palm ki taraf flex** kar dete hain.
- **Volar plate** bhi head ke volar side se juda hai, wo bhi flexion pull deta hai.
- Opposing force kaun hai? Proximal fragment ko extensors ka koi strong counter nahi milta, to head palm mein ghoom jaata hai.

**2. "Apex dorsal" ka matlab**
Head flex hokar palm ki taraf jhuka, to fracture site pe **kona (apex) dorsal side pe nikalta hai**. Isliye knuckle flat dikhta hai (head andar dhasa) aur dorsum pe hump aata hai. Head palm mein chubhta hai.

**3. Reduction hold kyun nahi hota (volar comminution)**
Jab mukka marte ho, axial force + flexion se **volar cortex pe compression** lagta hai, to wahi cortex crush/comminute ho jaata hai. Ab socho:
- Reduce karne pe fragment ko **volar side se support dene wali solid bone nahi hai** (wo toh kuchal chuki hai).
- Interossei ka pull wapas lagta hai, to head phir se flex hokar apex dorsal ban jaata hai.

Ek analogy: tent ka ek pole toot jaaye aur andar ka support kuchal jaaye. Tent ko seedha khada bhi kar do, par andar se roknewala kuch nahi hai, to wo wapas gir jaata hai.

**Practical matlab:**
- Isliye Jahss manoeuvre se reduce to ho jaata hai, par **cast mein bhi slip kar sakta hai**.
- Isliye jahan reduction chahiye aur hold nahi ho raha, wahan **pinning/fixation** lagta hai. K-wire ya bouquet pull ko mechanically rokta hai.
- Aur isliye hi **5th MC mein itni angulation accept hoti hai**, kyunki hold karwana mushkil hai aur CMC joint compensate kar leta hai.

Ek line mein yaad rakho: **Interossei/volar plate head ko palm mein kheenchte hain, volar bone kuchli hui hai to rok nahi paati, isliye apex dorsal deformity aati hai aur reduction slip karta hai.**

## Samajh: 30° semi-supinated lateral kaise lete hain

**30° semi-supinated lateral** matlab hand ko true lateral se thoda ghumake lena, taaki 5th MC apne neighbours (4th, 3rd) ke upar overlap na kare.

**Problem kyun hai:** Normal true lateral mein 2nd-5th metacarpals ek ke peeche ek superimpose ho jaate hain. 5th MC neck ka angulation measure karna impossible ho jaata hai.

**Kaise lena hai (X-ray technician ko ya fluoroscopy mein):**
1. Hand ko **ulnar border cassette/detector pe** rakho (true lateral position, hand pe side se).
2. Ab hand ko **forearm ke axis pe ~30° supinate karo**, yaani palm ko thoda upar ki taraf ghumao.
3. Beam vertical hi rahega, sirf hand ghumta hai.

**Isse kya hota hai:** 5th MC ulnar side pe thoda niche/alag ho jaata hai, aur 4th MC se overlap kam ho jaata hai, to 5th MC ka shaft aur neck alag dikhne lagta hai.

**Practical shortcut:** Fluoroscopy mein (OT mein) ye aasan hai. C-arm ghumao, hand nahi, aur dekho kab 5th MC alag dikhta hai.

**Angle measure kaise karte hain** (is view mein):
- Shaft ki mid-axis (2 points ka centre jod ke line).
- Head/neck ki axis.
- In dono lines ke beech ka angle = **neck angulation**.

**Note:** Kuch centres mein **Brewerton view** ya oblique use hota hai, par angulation ke liye ye semi-supinated lateral standard hai. Agar patient pain ki wajah se hand ghuma nahi paa raha, to jitna comfortable ho utna karo, aur zaroori ho to CT ya fluoro se confirm karo.

## Evidence check: functional treatment

**Mostly sahi, par maine thoda overstate kiya.**

**Jo sahi hai:**
- Statius Muller et al. (2003) ka RCT specifically 5th MC neck fractures pe tha: elastic compression bandage (functional) vs plaster cast. Final function aur cosmetic result mein koi significant difference nahi mila, aur functional group mein **recovery aur kaam pe wapsi jaldi** thi.
- Cochrane review (Poolman et al., 4th/5th MC neck fractures) bhi yahi kehta hai ki different conservative methods ke beech outcome mein clear difference nahi hai. Early mobilisation safe lagta hai.

**Jo maine zyada bol diya:**
- "Aaj ka preferred approach" thoda strong hai. Cochrane ne khud bola hai ki **trials chhote hain aur evidence quality low hai**, to ye "firm recommendation" nahi hai. Better wording: *functional treatment ek safe, evidence-supported option hai, aur cast se kam nahi*.
- Practice mein bahut jagah abhi bhi ulnar gutter splint/cast diya jaata hai (pain control, compliance, patient ki preference).

**Condition jo yaad rakho:** Ye sab sirf **closed, isolated, rotation normal, angulation acceptable** fractures ke liye hai. Scissoring, fight bite, multiple MC ya bahut zyada angulation mein ye logic apply nahi hota.

Exact numbers (patients, follow-up, outcome scores) memory se mat quote karna. Viva ya paper mein cite karne se pehle original paper ek baar check kar lena. Chaho to main search karke dono references verify kar deta hoon.

## Samajh: IP joint free ka matlab

**IP joint free** matlab cast/splint sirf **hand ke ulnar side + MCP tak** rahe, finger ke **PIP aur DIP joints (interphalangeal)** ko cast mein mat bandho. Wo khule rahein aur move karte rahein.

**Cast kahan tak aana chahiye:**
- Forearm ke ulnar side se shuru (proximal forearm/wrist ke paas)
- Hand ke ulnar border pe 4th aur 5th finger ke sath
- **Distal end: proximal phalanx ke mid-level tak**, PIP joint ke **pehle** tak. Uske aage ka finger bahar.

**Position:**
- Wrist ~20-30° extension
- **MCP 70-90° flexion** (collateral ligaments tight rehte hain, short hone ya stiff hone se bachte hain)
- PIP/DIP bandhna nahi hai, patient unhe actively flex-extend karta rahe.

**Kyun free rakhna hai:**
- PIP joint bahut jaldi stiff hota hai (flexion contracture), kyunki capsule aur volar plate immobilisation mein tight ho jaate hain.
- Fracture MC neck mein hai, IP joints ka isse koi lena dena nahi, to unhe bandhne ka fayda zero, nuksan zyada.
- Free IP joints se finger movement, tendon gliding aur swelling kam hoti hai, aur recovery tez hoti hai.

**Practical tip:** Patient ko bolo ki cast ke bahar finger ko roz kai baar poora flex aur extend kare (fist banana aur kholna). Cast ki edge PIP crease ko chhoo kar rukavat na kare. Wo flexion crease free hona chahiye.

**Ulnar gutter splint ka "gutter" kya hai:** Splint ulnar side pe gutter (naali) ki tarah wrap hota hai, jisme 4th-5th fingers rehte hain aur radial side (1st-3rd fingers) free rehti hai.

## Samajh: transverse intermetacarpal pinning aur tether

**Idea:** Fractured 5th MC ko stable 4th MC se pin karke "jod" dena, taaki fracture hilne na paaye. 4th MC ek splint ki tarah kaam karta hai.

**Kaise lagta hai:**
1. Pehle fracture reduce karo (Jahss ya fluoro mein).
2. 5th MC ke ulnar side se K-wire daalo, 4th MC ke andar tak (transverse direction mein, hand ke across).
3. **Ek wire fracture ke distal** (head/neck wala fragment pakadta hua), **ek wire fracture ke proximal** (shaft pe). Fracture dono wires ke beech mein aata hai.
4. Result: 5th MC ke dono fragments 4th MC se locked ho jaate hain, to head wapas flex hokar slip nahi kar sakta.

Ye "intermetacarpal pinning" isliye kehte hain kyunki wire **do metacarpals ke beech (inter)** guzarta hai.

**Tether ka matlab (risk):**
Ab 4th aur 5th MC aapas mein wire se bandhe hain, to dono **ek saath hi move kar sakte hain**. Normally 5th MC apne CMC joint pe alag se thoda move karta hai (opposition, grip cupping). Wire lagne se ye independent motion band ho jaati hai. Isse:
- Grip aur hand ka cupping thoda restrict hota hai jab tak wire hai
- 4th MC ke andar wire se irritation ya pin tract problem ho sakta hai
- Wire ke aas-paas interosseous muscle/tendon pe friction

Isliye wires **3-4 weeks mein nikaal dete hain**, aur uske baad motion wapas aa jaata hai.

**Kab prefer karte hain:** Simple, closed fracture, jahan quick aur easy fixation chahiye aur patient ko 3-4 week tak wires se issue nahi.

**Catch:** 4th MC pe wire jaane se uska bhi minor risk (infection, bone injury) aa jaata hai, jo tum bouquet technique mein avoid karte ho kyunki wo sirf 5th MC ke andar rehta hai.
