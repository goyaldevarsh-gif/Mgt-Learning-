Reference: Miller's Orthopaedic Sports Medicine, Campbell's, Rockwood & Green's. Ye working summary hai, book ka text nahi. Displacement thresholds aur brace protocols books aur authors mein alag milte hain, wahan flag kiya hai.

## 1. Basics
- PCL femur ki medial condyle se nikal ke **posterior tibial plateau (PCL facet)** pe lagta hai, joint line se ~1 cm neeche, extrasynovial.
- **Mechanism:**
  - **Dashboard injury:** flexed knee pe tibia ke upar se posterior force (RTA).
  - Sports mein fall on flexed knee, foot plantarflexed.
  - Hyperextension ya knee dislocation.
- **Isolated** avulsion kam hota hai. High-energy mein **combined injury** common hai: ACL, PLC, MCL, meniscus, plateau/fibular head fracture.
- **Midsubstance tear se difference:** avulsion mein bone-to-bone healing hoti hai, to fix karne pe result achha aata hai. Midsubstance PCL tear ligament elongation ke saath heal hoti hai.

## 2. Assessment

**History:** dashboard ya fall on flexed knee. Posterior knee pain, swelling, stairs aur downhill chalne mein dikkat.

**Inspection:** posterior knee ecchymosis, effusion (capsule tear ho to kam bhi ho sakti hai).

**Tests:**
1. **Posterior sag sign (Godfrey):** hip aur knee 90° flexion, tibia peeche sag karta hai.
2. **Posterior drawer at 90°:** sabse sensitive test. Normally medial tibial plateau medial femoral condyle se ~1 cm **aage** hota hai. Ye step-off kitna bacha, usse grade karo:

| Grade | Posterior translation | Step-off |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1-5 mm | Aage hai, par kam |
| II | 6-10 mm | Flush (barabar) |
| III | >10 mm | Peeche (combined injury suspect) |

3. **Quadriceps active test:** knee 90°, quads contract karwao. Tibia aage aata hai to PCL deficient.
4. **Dial test (30° aur 90°):**
   - Sirf 30° pe external rotation badhi = isolated PLC.
   - 30° aur 90° dono pe = PLC + PCL.
5. **Varus/valgus stress** (collaterals) aur **reverse pivot shift** (PLC).
6. **NV status:** popliteal artery, peroneal nerve. Combined injury ya dislocation suspect ho to **ABI** karo.

**Avulsion ka clue:** acute mein pain aur guarding ki wajah se drawer unreliable ho sakta hai, isliye imaging se confirm karo.

## 3. Imaging
- **X-ray AP + lateral:** lateral mein fragment posterior tibial plateau pe dikhta hai, aksar **proximally displaced** (PCL ne kheench liya). Ye lateral pe sabse achha dikhta hai.
- **Stress radiographs (Telos posterior stress at 90°):** side-to-side difference **>8 mm** complete PCL injury ki taraf, **>12 mm** tak combined/PLC suspect. Numbers authors mein alag hain, flag karo.
- **CT:** fragment size, comminution, displacement aur surgical planning ke liye. Displaced avulsion mein lagbhag hamesha lo.
- **MRI:** associated injuries (ACL, PLC, meniscus, chondral) aur PCL ki substance dekhne ke liye. Combined injury suspect ho to zaroor.

## 4. Management: kab konsa

**Nonoperative (isolated, undisplaced):**
- Displacement **<~2 mm** (kuch authors 5 mm tak accept karte hain, flag).
- **Hinged knee brace/cast in extension** with **posterior tibial support** (tibia peeche na sagge), ~4-6 weeks.
- **Active hamstring exercises avoid karo**, hamstring tibia ko peeche kheenchta hai.
- Phir prone passive ROM, quads strengthening.
- **1 week aur phir regular X-ray**, late displacement catch karne ke liye.

**Operative indications (yahi sabse zaroori):**
- **Displaced** fragment (>2 mm, ya rotated/inverted)
- **Large fragment** (fixation possible)
- **Combined ligament injuries** (ACL, PLC, MCL)
- **Residual laxity** after reduction attempt
- **Open injury**
- Young/athletic patient, instability

**Timing:** **Early surgery (<~2-3 weeks)** ideal. Chronic mein fragment malunite/nonunite ho jaata hai aur baad mein PCL reconstruction ki zarurat padti hai.

## 5. Operative techniques

**A. Open posterior approach (most classic)**
- **Patient prone** ya lateral decubitus.
- **Burks & Schaffer posteromedial approach** (ya Trickett modification): knee ke posteromedial side se, **medial head of gastrocnemius ko lateral retract** karo. Isse popliteal artery aur tibial nerve **gastrocnemius ke peeche protect** rehte hain.
- Posterior capsule incise karo, fragment expose karo.
- Bed clean karo, fragment **reduce** karo.
- **Fixation:** 4.0 mm cancellous screw with washer (1 ya 2), ya suture/anchor, ya suture bridge. Chhote ya comminuted fragment mein sutures.
- Fluoroscopy se reduction aur screw length confirm karo.
- **Risk:** popliteal artery, tibial nerve, screw ki length.

**B. Arthroscopic fixation**
- **Posteromedial/posterolateral portals**, kabhi trans-septal portal.
- Fragment reduce karo, **suture fixation** (PCL/fragment mein high-strength sutures, anterior tibial tunnel se bahar nikaal ke button ya bone bridge pe tie karo) ya cannulated screw.
- **Pros:** NV structures se door, associated pathology bhi treat. **Cons:** technically demanding, learning curve.

**Choose kaise:** simple large fragment aur experience kam = open posterior. Experienced arthroscopist, chhota fragment, combined injury = arthroscopic.

## 6. Post-op protocol (general)
- Hinged brace **extension mein, posterior support** ke saath, 4-6 weeks.
- **Early prone passive flexion** (gravity-assisted), limited arc, fixation stability ke hisaab se.
- **Active hamstring / resisted flexion 6-8 weeks tak avoid.**
- Quads strengthening, closed-chain exercises, proprioception.
- **Return to sport ~4-6 months**, strength aur stability regain hone pe. Protocol surgeon ke hisaab se vary karta hai.

## 7. Complications
- **Residual posterior laxity** (union ke baad bhi, kam symptomatic)
- Nonunion/malunion, loss of fixation
- **Arthrofibrosis / flexion loss**
- **Popliteal artery/tibial nerve injury** (open approach)
- Hardware irritation
- Chronic instability, baad mein reconstruction

## 8. Quick rules (OPD/OT) {revise}
1. Dashboard/flexed knee fall + posterior sag = **PCL suspect**.
2. **Lateral X-ray zaroor dekho**, fragment wahin dikhta hai.
3. Displaced ya combined = **CT/MRI + surgery**.
4. Undisplaced isolated = brace in extension with **posterior support**, hamstring avoid.
5. Surgery mein **gastrocnemius ko protector** ki tarah use karo (open approach).
6. Early fix = bone-to-bone healing, achha result.
