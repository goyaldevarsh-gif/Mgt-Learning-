## 1. Thromboembolism

- Without prophylaxis DVT **1-84%**, proximal 0-16%; symptomatic PE 0-1%, fatal 0-1.5% [[p.458]]
- **Gold standard = venography**; duplex is operator dependent (52-86%)

| Prophylaxis | Note |
|---|---|
| Mechanical | Safe; compliance limited |
| LMWH / fondaparinux | Effective; **epidural haematoma risk**, highest at catheter removal (POD 3) |
| ACCP 2008 | LMWH, fondaparinux or VKA for >= **10 days** |
| Xa inhibitors | RECORD: fewer DVT than enoxaparin, more wound problems |

> [!samajh] Trade-off hamesha **DVT kam vs wound/bleeding**. Book koi ek drug final nahi bolta.

## 2. Infection: diagnosis

- Risk: RA, skin ulcer, previous surgery, **hinge**, obesity, UTI, steroids, DM, malnutrition, psoriasis [[p.459]]
- Prevention: **vertical** laminar flow (horizontal increased infection), cefazolin [[p.460]]
- **CRP is more reliable than ESR/WBC.** Aspirate (stop antibiotics 2 weeks): **WBC > 2500, PMN >= 60%** [[p.460]]

:::flow
[teal] Painful TKA | Suspect infection [[p.460]]
v
ESR + CRP | CRP more reliable
v
Aspirate | Cell count + culture, stop antibiotics 2 wks
v
[amber] MSIS score | >= 6 infected; 3-5 inconclusive; < 3 not infected [[p.462]]
:::

| MSIS item | Chronic | Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Major (any one):** 2 positive cultures same organism, or sinus tract | - | Infected |
| Serum CRP / D-dimer | 10 mg/L / 860 | 2 |
| Serum ESR | 30 | 1 |
| Synovial WBC | 3000 | 3 |
| Synovial PMN % | 70 (acute 90) | 2 |
| Single positive culture | - | 2 |
| Positive histology / purulence | - | 3 each |

## 3. Infection: treatment ladder

:::flow
[teal] Early (< 4 wks) or acute haematogenous + well-fixed | Debridement + poly exchange, 6 wks IV antibiotics [[p.462]]
v
[red] Chronic (> 4 wks, insidious) | DAIR universally unsuccessful, remove implant
v
Two-stage exchange | 6 wks IV, titer 1:8, then reimplant ;; Arthrodesis | High demand, poor extensor or soft tissue
v
Resection arthroplasty | Polyarticular RA, low demand ;; AKA | Last option
:::

- Fusion: **IM nail 95% vs ex-fix 64%**; gram-positive 100% vs 73% [[p.463]]
- Antibiotic spacer: 3.6 g tobramycin + 3 g vancomycin per pack; articulating spacer 88-96% success, 100-104 deg flexion
- Parvizi: **mortality 13.9%** in 570 PJI patients [[p.464]]

Early (< 4 wks) or acute haematogenous with well-fixed implant. Chronic insidious = never [[p.462]].
:::

## 4. Patellofemoral complications

![Fig 7.74: Lateral patellar subluxation on skyline view](figs/f7_74.jpg)

- **Instability:** tight lateral retinaculum, malpositioned components; close retinaculum at **90 deg**; lateral facet resection **shallower than medial** [[p.465]]
- Fix order: correct components, lateral release, proximal realignment; **distal realignment (tubercle) only with extreme caution** [[p.466]]

| Patellar fracture (< 1%) | Type | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Extensor intact, implant stable | Immobiliser / cast 6 wks |
| **II** | Extensor discontinuity | Tension band (transverse), partial patellectomy (pole) |
| **III** | Loose patellar component | Remove component, do not replace |

- **Patellar clunk:** nodule on posterior quad tendon catches in the notch at **30-45 deg**; **arthroscopic debridement** [[p.467]]
- **Extensor rupture (0.1-0.55%):** partial = non-op; complete = repair (poor results); options: tension band + hamstring augment, **extensor allograft**, gastrocnemius flap

## 5. Nerve, vessel, fractures

- **Arterial:** 0.03-0.2%, **25% end in amputation**
- **Peroneal palsy:** < 1-2%; **fixed valgus + flexion contracture** correction; if seen **release dressing and flex the knee** [[p.468]]
- **Supracondylar femoral fracture:** 0.3-2%; notching, osteoporosis, RA, steroids [[p.468]]

| Rorabeck | Description | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| I | Undisplaced, prosthesis stable | Non-op |
| II | Displaced, prosthesis stable | Fix (locked plate, retrograde nail) |
| III | Prosthesis loose | Revision, long stem |
