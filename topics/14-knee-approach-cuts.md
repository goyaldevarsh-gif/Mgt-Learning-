## 1. Approaches

- **Skin:** midline anterior, knee flexed. Use the **most lateral usable old scar** (blood supply is from medial). Saphenous infrapatellar branch is cut, so lateral numbness is expected: warn patient [[p.436]]
- **Standard deep approach = medial parapatellar** (Technique 7.1): 3-4 mm cuff on vastus medialis, around patella, 3-4 cm onto tibia, subperiosteal medial release [[p.436]]
- **Tibia subluxed + externally rotated** = extensor mechanism relaxes. **Patellar tendon avulsion is devastating** and hard to repair

![Fig 7.35: Medial parapatellar retinacular approach](figs/f7_35.jpg)

| Approach | Idea | Plus | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subvastus** | Lift vastus medialis off intermuscular septum | Quadriceps recovery, patellar blood supply, less lateral release | Obese, previously operated knees |
| **Midvastus** | Split vastus medialis along fibres, safe zone 4.5 cm | Spares supreme genicular artery and quad tendon | Obesity, previous HTO, flexion < 80 deg |

![Fig 7.38: Subvastus approach](figs/f7_38.jpg)

> [!samajh] Choice
> **Exposure vs quadriceps preserve.** Medial parapatellar = sabse zyada exposure, isliye default. Obese ya stiff knee mein sub/midvastus mushkil.

## 2. IM vs EM guides

- **Femur: IM** (no palpable landmark). Entry point can change sagittal cut by up to **5 deg** [[p.437]]
- EM femoral only for bowing, malunion, hardware. **ASIS unreliable** for hip centre
- **Tibia: EM** preferred by the authors; IM less reliable in **bowed tibia** (valgus knees: only 37% neutral vs 83% in varus) [[p.438]]
- **Fat embolism:** vent canal with **12.7 mm hole + 8 mm fluted rod** eliminated cardiopulmonary effects [[p.437]]
- Ankle centre ~**2 mm medial** to malleolar midpoint

![Fig 7.40: Bowed tibia precludes IM guide, use EM (B)](figs/f7_40.jpg)

## 3. Bone cuts (Technique 7.2)

- **Distal femur:** valgus **5-7 deg**; for flexion contracture cut extra but joint line **not > 4 mm** up; PS: extra **2 mm** [[p.439]]
- **Tibia:** perpendicular to mechanical axis; ~3 deg slope often built into insert; ~8-10 mm from good side, <= 2 mm from worse side

| Rotation reference | Pitfall |
|---|---|
| Transepicondylar axis | Hard to find |
| AP (Whiteside) axis | Dysplastic trochlea |
| Posterior condyles + 3 deg ER | Hypoplastic lateral condyle gives internal rotation |
| Tibial cut (gap technique) | Needs equal tension |

![Fig 7.42: Hypoplastic lateral condyle: posterior condylar axis gives relative internal rotation; AP axis is correct](figs/f7_42.jpg)

![Fig 7.43: Rectangular flexion gap by externally rotating the femoral cutting jig](figs/f7_43.jpg)

> [!flag] Femoral rotation galat = flexion gap trapezoid (ER zyada = medial gap khulta) ya patellar tilt (IR). Hamesha **ek se zyada reference** use karo.

> [!samajh] Anterior vs posterior referencing
> **Posterior referencing:** posterior cut fixed, size error **aage** dikhta hai (notch / overstuff). **Anterior referencing:** anterior cut fixed, error **peeche** dikhta hai (size bada = flexion gap tight). Isliye "chhota size + anterior referencing" = flexion gap badhane ka tareeka.

## 4. Gap balancing rule

> [!tip] **Distal femur cut = extension gap. Posterior femur cut = flexion gap. Tibial cut = dono.**

:::flow
[teal] Remove osteophytes first | posterior osteophytes tent capsule [[p.441]]
v
Ext gap < flexion gap | More distal femur ;; Flexion gap < ext gap | Smaller femoral size, anterior referencing
v
[amber] Gaps equal, space kam | More proximal tibia ;; Gaps equal, lax | Thicker poly
:::

:::qa Flexion gap tight, extension ok. Fix?
Next smaller femoral component with anterior referencing (shortens posterior condyles) [[p.441]].
:::

:::qa Gaps equal but both tight?
Cut more proximal tibia [[p.442]].
:::

## 5. Navigation, robotics, custom blocks

![Fig 7.45: Image-free navigation with femoral and tibial trackers](figs/f7_45.jpg)

| Tech | Plus | Minus |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | Fewer coronal outliers; avoids IM canal; good with hardware/deformity (Fig 7.46) | Cost, time, pin-hole fractures, no survivorship data |
| Robotic | Haptics, real-time planning | No proven better outcome, cost |
| Custom blocks (MRI/CT) | Planning outside OR | Cannot confirm cut is right until post-op X-ray |

- Meta-analysis (22 studies): better alignment, **no better function** [[p.443]]
