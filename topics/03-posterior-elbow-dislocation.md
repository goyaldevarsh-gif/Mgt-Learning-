Reference: Rockwood & Green's Fractures in Adults, Morrey's The Elbow, Campbell's. Ye working summary hai, book ka text nahi. Exact time limits aur angles books mein thode alag milte hain, jahan zaroori hai wahan flag kiya hai.

## 1. Basics
- Second most common major joint dislocation in adults (after shoulder). Posterior = ~90% of elbow dislocations.
- **Mechanism:** FOOSH with elbow near extension, axial load + valgus + supination (posterolateral rotatory mechanism).
- **Simple** = no fracture (~50-60%). **Complex** = with fracture (radial head, coronoid, olecranon, epicondyle).

## 2. Stability anatomy (yeh samajh liya to poora topic samajh aa gaya)
- **Bony:** ulnohumeral joint (coronoid + olecranon) = main stabiliser.
- **Medial:** MCL anterior bundle (valgus ka primary restraint).
- **Lateral:** LUCL (posterolateral rotatory stability).
- **Secondary:** radial head, capsule, common flexor/extensor origins.
- **Injury sequence (Horii/O'Driscoll circle):** soft tissue injury **lateral se medial** jaati hai: LUCL, then capsule, then MCL. Dislocation ke time kam se kam LUCL + capsule phatte hain, aksar MCL bhi.

## 3. Assessment
- Deformity, prominent olecranon, **triangle sign lost** (epicondyles + olecranon).
- **NV status pehle aur reduction ke baad dono baar document karo:** radial/brachial pulse, cap refill, median (AIN), ulnar nerve. Forearm compartments check karo.
- Wrist/forearm/shoulder check karo (**Essex-Lopresti**, distal radius, clavicle).
- Open wound = open dislocation.
- **X-ray pre-reduction:** AP + lateral. Fleck sign (avulsed LCL/coronoid) dhoondo.
- **CT (3D)** agar fracture ho ya concentric reduction pe doubt ho. MRI routine nahi.

## 4. Reduction
1. Analgesia/sedation (ya intra-articular lidocaine).
2. Assistant arm pe **countertraction**, tum wrist se **longitudinal traction** (slight flexion).
3. **Pehle medial/lateral displacement correct karo** (warna coronoid humerus pe atak jaata hai).
4. Phir olecranon pe **distal + anterior** pressure dete hue flex karo. "Clunk" feel hoga.
- **Prone method:** patient prone, arm table ke kinare se latka hua, weight se gravity reduce karti hai.
- **Hyperextension se avoid karo** (coronoid fracture/NV risk).
- Reduction ke baad **dobara NV check**, phir X-ray.

## 5. Post-reduction stability check (sabse important step)
**Pehle X-ray:** lateral mein ulnohumeral joint parallel aur concentric hona chahiye. Agar joint space widen hai (**"drop sign"**), ya subluxation dikhta hai, to instability hai.

**Phir clinically/fluoroscopy mein (sedation mein):**
1. Elbow ko poore arc mein **flex-extend** karo, dekho kis angle pe subluxate hota hai.
2. **Forearm rotation ke saath test karo:**
   - LCL deficient → **pronation** mein stable.
   - MCL deficient → **supination** mein stable.
   - Dono deficient → neutral mein stable.
3. **Varus/valgus stress** aur **lateral pivot-shift** (awake mein mushkil, sedation mein reliable).
4. Terminal extension tak concentric rehta hai to **stable**.

**Samajhne ka rule:**
| Finding | Matlab |
|---|---|
| Poore arc mein concentric | Stable, early motion |
| Sirf terminal extension (~30° ke aas-paas) mein subluxate | Mild instability, hinged brace with extension block |
| Mid-arc (~45°+ extension) pe bhi subluxate / flexion >90° chahiye | Unstable, surgery |

> [!verify] Flag: exact degrees (30° vs 45°) alag authors alag dete hain. Principle yaad rakho, number nahi.

## 6. Management

### A. Simple + stable (aam case)
- **Short immobilisation:** posterior above-elbow slab ya sling, **90° flexion**, **5-7 days (max ~10 days)**.
- Phir **active ROM** shuru, **hinged brace/sling** comfort ke liye.
- **Forearm position exercise ke time:**
  - LCL injury → pronation, **arm overhead** (gravity varus stress kam).
  - MCL injury → supination, arm side pe.
- Evidence: Iordens et al. (Dutch RCT) ne dikhaya ki early mobilisation se short-term function achha aata hai aur redislocation zyada nahi hota. (Original paper verify kar lena cite karne se pehle.)
- **Timeline:** week 1 slab, week 1-6 active/active-assisted ROM, week 6+ strengthening, contact sports ~3 months.
- **Passive stretching/forced manipulation mat karo** (stiffness + HO).
- **Weekly X-ray 1-2 weeks tak** (redisplacement check).
- **3 weeks se zyada immobilise mat karo.** Stiffness ka sabse bada reason yahi hai.

### B. Simple + unstable
Options (stability ke hisaab se):
1. **Cast/brace with extension block** (jis angle pe stable hai), har week 10-15° extension badhao, 4-6 weeks.
2. **LCL repair ± common extensor origin** (Kocher/lateral approach). Anchor ya transosseous suture se humeral origin pe.
3. Agar phir bhi unstable: **MCL repair** (medial approach).
4. Agar abhi bhi unstable: **hinged external fixator** (static ya dynamic) ya internal joint stabiliser.
- *Sequence yaad rakho:* lateral → coronoid/radial head → LCL → MCL → fixator.

### C. Complex (fracture-dislocation)
- **Terrible triad** (posterior dislocation + radial head fx + coronoid fx): 
  - Coronoid fix (screw / suture lasso / buttress plate)
  - Radial head: fix ya **replace** (3+ fragment unreconstructable). **Excision nahi.**
  - LCL repair ± MCL repair, ± fixator.
- **Posteromedial rotational instability** (anteromedial facet coronoid fx): medial buttress plate.
- **Olecranon fracture-dislocation:** posterior plate fixation.
- **Essex-Lopresti:** radial head kabhi excise mat karo.
- Complex wale mein bhi **early motion** (stable fixation ke baad) ka target rakho.

## 7. Open reduction kab
- Reduction nahi ho raha (tissue/fragment interposed).
- Brachial artery/median nerve entrapment.
- Open dislocation.
- Loss of concentric reduction after attempts.

## 8. Complications
- **Stiffness** (most common, terminal extension loss).
- **Heterotopic ossification** (delayed reduction, forceful physio, head injury).
- Recurrent/chronic instability (simple mein rare).
- Ulnar neuropathy, brachial artery injury, compartment syndrome.
- Post-traumatic arthritis.

## 9. Quick rules for OPD/OT {revise}
1. NV status **reduction se pehle aur baad** dono baar.
2. Post-reduction X-ray mein **concentric?** drop sign?
3. **Stability test with forearm rotation** zaroor karo.
4. Stable simple = **chhota immobilisation + early ROM**.
5. Unstable ya fracture = **CT, plan surgery**.
6. Kabhi bhi long immobilisation mat karo, stiffness aati hai.

## Samajh: Horii / O'Driscoll circle

Ye elbow ko ek **circle** ki tarah socho. Force lateral side se shuru hoke medial side tak jaata hai, aur har stage mein ek aur structure phat'ta hai.

**Mechanism:** axial load + valgus + supination (posterolateral rotatory force). Ulna humerus se bahar ki taraf ghoomti hai, isliye **sabse pehle lateral side (LUCL) phat'ti hai**.

**Stages (O'Driscoll):**
1. **Stage 1:** LUCL phati. Posterolateral subluxation hota hai, par khud reduce ho jaata hai.
2. **Stage 2:** LUCL ke saath anterior aur posterior capsule bhi phati. Coronoid trochlea ke neeche "perch" kar jaata hai, incomplete dislocation.
3. **Stage 3 (MCL ki bari):**
   - **3A:** sirf MCL ka **posterior band** phata, anterior band intact. Dislocation hota hai, par reduction ke baad elbow aksar stable rehta hai.
   - **3B:** poora MCL phata (anterior band bhi). Valgus instability aati hai.
   - **3C:** poora distal humerus soft tissue se strip ho jaata hai (flexor + extensor origins bhi). Reduction ke baad bhi bahut unstable, aksar fixator tak baat jaati hai.

**Isme se kya yaad rakhna hai:**
- Dislocation hone ke liye **kam se kam Stage 2** chahiye (LUCL + capsule). Isliye har dislocation mein LUCL phati hoti hai.
- MCL har case mein poori nahi phat'ti. 3A mein sirf posterior band phat'ta hai. Isliye maine "aksar MCL" bola tha, "hamesha" nahi.
- Jitna zyada medial side damage, utni zyada instability.

**Practical use:**
- **Stability test:** LCL deficient to pronation mein stable, MCL deficient to supination mein stable. Ye circle ka hi direct application hai.
- **Surgery sequence:** lateral se shuru karo (LCL repair), phir zaroorat pade to MCL. Wahi order jisme chot lagi thi.
- **Rehab:** LCL injury mein pronation + arm overhead, kyunki wahi side pehle phati hai.

**Ek caveat:** Ye O'Driscoll ka theoretical model hai. Baad ki MRI aur surgical studies mein har case is order mein nahi mila. Kuch mein LCL intact thi ya MCL pehle phati. Isliye ise ek guide samjho, har patient pe fix rule nahi. Hamesha clinically aur fluoroscopy mein stability check karo.

## Samajh: concentric reduction aur drop sign

**Concentric** matlab ulna ka trochlear notch humerus ke trochlea pe ek dum fit baitha hai, aur dono ke beech joint space **poore arc mein barabar** (uniform) hai.

**Lateral X-ray mein kya dekhna hai:**
- Humeral trochlea (gol pulley) aur ulna ka trochlear notch ek dusre ke **parallel curve** follow karein.
- Joint space har jagah same width ka dikhe, koi jagah gap nahi.
- Ulna humerus ke neeche seedha baitha ho, na aage na peeche khisaka hua.

Isse pata chalta hai ki reduction sahi hua aur soft tissue joint ko hold kar raha hai.

**Drop sign kya hai:**
Trochlea aur trochlear notch ke beech ka space **kahin widen** ho jaata hai (ek taraf gap, doosri taraf contact). Matlab ulna ne humerus ko poora nahi pakda, wo "latka" hua ya thoda khisaka hua hai. Isse bolte hain ulnohumeral joint **incongruent/widened**.

**Ye kyun hota hai:**
- LCL aur capsule phati hone se ulna ko hold karne wala stabiliser nahi bacha.
- Ya coronoid fracture hai (bony buttress gaya).
- Ya joint mein koi tissue/fragment phansa hai (interposition), to bone poora baith nahi paa raha.
- Kabhi kabhi **sirf muscle ke relaxation mein gravity** se bhi gap dikhta hai (sedation/anaesthesia mein), jo ek tarah ki instability ka hi sign hai.

**Matlab kya nikalta hai:**
- Concentric + uniform space = reduction acha, stable hone ke chances high. Aage stability test karo.
- Widened/drop sign ya subluxation = **unstable ya incomplete reduction.** Fracture ya interposed tissue dhoondo, **CT karo**, aur simple + conservative plan mat banao.

**Ek important baat:** Ye X-ray sirf ek check hai. Concentric dikhne ka matlab ye nahi ki elbow stable hai. Phir bhi **flexion-extension arc mein stability test** (forearm rotation ke saath) zaroor karo. Aur X-ray repeat karo 1 week pe, kyunki late subluxation aa sakta hai.

## Samajh: rehab mein forearm aur arm position

Ye rehab ke time ka rule hai: jab patient active ROM kar raha ho, to forearm ki position aur arm ki position aisi rakho ki **injured ligament pe stress na pade**.

**LCL injury (lateral side)**
- LCL ka kaam: varus aur posterolateral rotatory instability rokna.
- **Pronation** mein lateral side ke structures (common extensor origin + LCL) relax aur protected rehte hain, to elbow stable rehta hai.
- **Arm overhead** (shoulder flexed ~90°+, elbow upar) rakhne se **gravity varus force ko kam** kar deti hai. Normally arm side pe latakta hai to gravity elbow ko varus mein kheenchti hai, jo injured LCL ko stress deta hai. Overhead position mein ye reverse ho jaata hai.
- Practical: patient lete hue (supine) shoulder flexed karke, elbow chhat ki taraf, palm niche (pronated), phir elbow flex-extend kare.

**MCL injury (medial side)**
- MCL ka kaam: valgus stability.
- **Supination** mein medial structures (flexor-pronator origin) protected hote hain aur MCL pe tension kam hoti hai.
- **Arm side pe** (body ke paas) rakhne se gravity valgus stress nahi deti, to ligament safe rehti hai. Overhead mein valgus stress badh sakta hai.
- Practical: arm body ke paas, palm upar (supinated), elbow flex-extend.

**Dono ek saath (zyada tar dislocation mein)**
- Dono injured hain to **neutral rotation** (na pura pronate, na supinate) mein exercise karo, aur hinged brace use karo.
- Aur jis angle pe subluxate hota hai, wahan extension block lagao.

**Yaad rakhne ka shortcut:**
- **L**CL = **P**ronation + **O**verhead
- **M**CL = **S**upination + **S**ide

**Caveat:** Ye position-wise protocol practice mein broadly followed hai, par exact evidence limited hai. Individual patient mein stability test ke hisaab se adjust karo.

## LCL ya MCL gaya, pata kaise lagega

Short answer: dislocation hua hai to **LCL almost hamesha gaya hai** (circle ke hisaab se pehle wahi phat'ti hai). Asli sawaal ye hai ki **MCL bhi gaya ya nahi**, aur wo stability test se pata lagta hai.

**1. Pehle history/exam (reduction ke baad)**
- **Lateral side tenderness/swelling/ecchymosis:** lateral epicondyle ke paas (LUCL origin) = LCL injury.
- **Medial side tenderness/ecchymosis:** medial epicondyle ke paas = MCL injury.
- Dislocation ke baad dono taraf tenderness aam hai, to akele ispe bharosa mat karo.

**2. Stability test (sabse reliable, sedation ya awake agar patient tolerate kare)**
- **Pronation vs supination test (final, corrected rule):**
  - **LCL deficient → pronation mein stable, supination mein unstable.**
  - **MCL deficient → supination mein stable, pronation mein unstable.**
  - Dono positions mein unstable = dono ligaments gaye, ya bony problem (coronoid).
- **Valgus stress test:** elbow ~30° flexion mein, forearm **pronated**, valgus force lagao. Medial side pe gap khulta hai to **MCL incompetent**.
- **Varus stress test:** 30° flexion mein, forearm pronated, varus force. Lateral gap = LCL/lateral structures incompetent.
- **Lateral pivot-shift test (O'Driscoll):** supine, arm overhead, forearm supinated, valgus + axial load, elbow flex karo. ~40° pe subluxation, aur 40-70° ke beech sudden reduction ("clunk") = **posterolateral rotatory instability (LCL deficient)**. Awake patient mein pain/apprehension ki wajah se mushkil, anaesthesia mein reliable.
- **Moving valgus stress test** (O'Driscoll, valgus instability ke liye): shoulder abducted, elbow flexed, valgus lagake extend karo. 70-120° pe pain = MCL injury (zyada overhead throwers mein).

**3. Fluoroscopy (OT mein, sedation ke saath)**
- Stress under live fluoro, ulnohumeral aur radiocapitellar joint ka gapping dekho.
- Valgus stress pe medial joint gap > ~2-3 mm opposite side ke comparison mein = MCL injury. (Exact mm numbers alag papers mein alag hain, comparison zaroor karo.)

**4. MRI (agar clinical aur fluoro se clear nahi)**
- MCL aur LUCL dono ke tear aur grade dikhata hai. Routine nahi, sirf doubt mein ya chronic instability mein.

**Practical rule:**
1. Dislocation = LCL gayi manke chalo.
2. Reduction ke baad **supination aur pronation dono mein arc test** karo.
3. Pronation mein bhi unstable ya valgus stress pe gapping = **MCL bhi gayi**, to surgery ya hinged fixator ki taraf socho.
4. Pronation mein stable, supination mein unstable = sirf LCL, to hinged brace pronation mein.

**Caveat:** Awake patient mein pain aur guarding ki wajah se ye tests unreliable hote hain. Isliye accuracy ke liye anaesthesia/sedation mein fluoro ke saath karo.
