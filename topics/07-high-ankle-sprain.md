Reference: Kellett, Hunt (OKU), van Dijk/ESSKA-AFAS syndesmosis consensus, Campbell's, Rockwood & Green's. Ye working summary hai, book ka text nahi. Mm/degree cutoffs aur timelines authors mein alag hain, wahan flag kiya hai.

## 1. Anatomy: kya hold karta hai
Syndesmosis = distal tibia aur fibula ka fibrous joint, jo **ankle mortise ko tight rakhta hai** taaki talus fit rahe.

| Structure | Role |
|---|---|
| **AITFL** (anterior inferior tibiofibular) | Sabse commonly injured, pehle phat'ta hai |
| **PITFL** (posterior inferior) | Strongest, aur injury mein baad mein jaata hai |
| **Interosseous ligament (IOL)** | Syndesmosis ke beech ka ligament |
| **Interosseous membrane (IOM)** | Tibia-fibula ke beech ki sheet, upar tak jaati hai |
| **Transverse tibiofibular ligament** | PITFL ka deep part |
| **Deltoid ligament** | Medial stabiliser, syndesmosis injury mein aksar saath mein hurt |

> [!verify] Relative contribution (approx, flag): PITFL ~40%, AITFL ~35%, IOL ~22%. Papers mein numbers alag hain.

**Mortise ko samjho (Hinglish):** Talus ek wedge hai, aage se chauda. Dorsiflexion mein ye mortise mein fit hota hai aur fibula ko thoda bahar dhakelta hai. Syndesmosis hi fibula ko wapas tibia ke paas khinche rakhta hai. Ye phata to talus ko jagah mil jaati hai aur wo mortise mein loose ho jaata hai.

## 2. Mechanism
- **Dorsiflexion + external rotation** of the foot (sabse common): football, rugby, skiing, wrestling. Foot planted hai, body ghoom jaati hai.
- Talus externally rotate hokar fibula ko tibia se **bahar dhakelta** hai, to AITFL → IOL → IOM → PITFL **distal se proximal** phat'te hain.
- **Pronation + ER** (deltoid pehle fail, phir syndesmosis) bhi hota hai.
- Fibula fracture ke saath ho to **Weber C / Lauge-Hansen SER/PER** pattern, jo ab fracture ki category ban jaati hai.

**Frequency:** ankle sprains mein ~1-18% (range wide hai, flag). Contact sports mein zyada. **Aksar miss hota hai**, kyunki log use lateral sprain samajh lete hain.

## 3. Associated injuries (check karo)
- **Deltoid ligament** (medial tenderness, ecchymosis)
- **Fibula fracture** (Weber C / **Maisonneuve**: proximal fibula tak force jaati hai)
- **Posterior malleolus fracture** (PITFL avulsion)
- **Osteochondral lesion of talus**
- Lateral ligament (ATFL) injury bhi saath ho sakta hai
- Peroneal tendon, anterior process calcaneus

## 4. Clinical assessment

**History:** twisting mechanism (ER/dorsiflexion), weight-bear mein pain, **push-off aur cutting pe pain**, anterior ankle ke upar dard (lateral malleolus ke neeche nahi).

**Inspection:** swelling zyada nahi bhi ho sakti (lateral sprain jaisi nahi). Ecchymosis late dikhta hai (calf tak).

**Palpation:**
- **Anterior syndesmosis (AITFL)** tenderness, ankle joint line ke **upar**.
- **Medial deltoid** tenderness.
- **Proximal fibula + IOM** tenderness (Maisonneuve rule out).
- **Length of tenderness:** syndesmosis tenderness ki length lambi ho (e.g., >~6 cm above joint line) to zyada severity. Flag.

**Special tests (step-by-step maine pehle diya, yahan purpose aur reliability):**
| Test | Positive finding | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Squeeze** | Distal syndesmosis pain jab mid-calf pe tibia-fibula squeeze karte ho | Specific, sensitivity limited |
| **External rotation (Kleiger)** | Anterior syndesmosis ya medial pain | Most useful |
| **Cotton (lateral translation)** | Fibula ka excess lateral shift | Fluoroscopy se better |
| **Dorsiflexion-compression test** | Dorsiflexion + compression pe pain | Reasonably good |
| **Crossed-leg test** | Cross-leg position pe pain | Alternate |
| **Stabilisation test** | Taping/stabilisation se pain kam | Hopping/functional |
| **Fibular translation test** | AP translation of fibula, side to side | Limited |

**Practical rule:** koi bhi ek test se rule out mat karo. **Mechanism + tenderness + 2-3 tests** milake dekho. **Acute mein pain ke karan sab unreliable** hain, to imaging zaroori hai.

## 5. Imaging

### X-ray (weight-bearing zaroor, agar possible ho)
Views: **AP, mortise, lateral**. **Contralateral comparison** useful hai.

**Measurements (AP/mortise, tibial plafond se ~1 cm upar):**
| Measurement | Abnormal (flag, authors vary) |
|---|---|
| **Tibiofibular clear space (TFCS)** | **>6 mm** (AP/mortise) |
| **Tibiofibular overlap (TFO)** | **<6 mm on AP, <1 mm on mortise** |
| **Medial clear space (MCS)** | **>4 mm** (deltoid/mortise instability) |

**Note:** Ye numbers rotation aur projection se badal jaate hain, aur 40-50% syndesmotic injuries mein plain X-ray **normal** hota hai (**latent/dynamic instability**). To normal X-ray = rule out nahi.

- **Stress views:** external rotation stress ya **gravity stress** X-ray, fluoroscopic Cotton/ER stress.
- **Weight-bearing** view dynamic widening dikha deta hai.

### MRI
- AITFL/PITFL/IOL/IOM ka **tear, grade, aur associated injuries** (deltoid, OLT, bone bruise, fracture) dikhata hai.
- Sensitivity/specificity high, par **stability pe MRI akela decide nahi karta**.
- Sports athletes mein baseline workup ka part hai.

### CT
- **Bilateral weight-bearing CT** ya bilateral CT: subtle diastasis, rotational malalignment, posterior malleolus, malreduction (post-op) ke liye **best**. **Bilateral comparison** zaroori hai kyunki normal anatomy variable hai.

### Ultrasound (dynamic) aur arthroscopy
- Dynamic USG ek option hai (operator-dependent).
- **Arthroscopy = gold standard** instability confirm karne ke liye (talus ko tibia-fibula ke beech nikalne wala "drive-through"/probe test, gap >2 mm flag).

## 6. Classification

### West Point grading (sports, stability-based, practical)
| Grade | Injury | Stability | Typical plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | AITFL sprain/partial | Stable | Functional/boot |
| **II** | AITFL + IOL partial tear, ± deltoid | **Stable to equivocal** | Protected WB, rehab; stress/MRI se decide |
| **III** | Complete syndesmosis tear (AITFL, IOL, PITFL), ± deltoid | **Unstable** | **Surgery** |

### Edwards & DeLee (diastasis pattern, fracture + syndesmosis ke liye zyada)
- Type I: latent diastasis (stress pe hi dikhta hai)
- Type II: frank diastasis, no fracture
- Type III-IV: plastic deformation / fracture-associated

## 7. Management decision (core)

**Pehle sawal: stable ya unstable?**
- **Stable:** X-ray normal + stress view normal, MRI mein partial tear.
- **Unstable/doubt:** frank diastasis, latent diastasis on stress, complete tear MRI mein, ya fracture + syndesmosis (Weber C).

### A. Stable (West Point I-II)
- **Protect:** boot/air cast ~**1-2 weeks** (severe mein ~2-6 weeks), protected weight-bearing as tolerated.
- **PEACE & LOVE** principles: early controlled loading, pain-free ROM.
- **Rehab phases:**
  1. Swelling/pain control, ROM (dorsiflexion kam rakho avoid mein stress), isometrics.
  2. Strengthening (calf, peroneals, tibialis), balance/proprioception.
  3. Running progression, agility, cutting.
  4. Sport-specific, **taping/brace**.
- **Avoid early:** forceful dorsiflexion + ER, aggressive stretching, jumping.
- **Return to sport:** Grade I ~**2-4 weeks**, Grade II ~**4-8 weeks** (flag). Pain-free hopping/cutting criteria pe based, calendar pe nahi.
- **Follow-up X-ray** (weight bearing) 1-2 weeks aur phir, widening late bhi aa sakti hai.

### B. Unstable (West Point III, diastasis, Weber C with syndesmosis)
**Operative:** reduction + fixation. **Reduction ki quality hi outcome decide karti hai.**

**Techniques:**
| Method | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Syndesmotic screw (3.5 or 4.5 mm, 1-2 screws, 3 ya 4 cortex)** | Rigid, long track record | Rigid fixation (physiologic fibular motion nahi), **screw breakage/loosening**, removal debate, late WB |
| **Suture-button (TightRope, Arthrex etc.)** | **Flexible**, physiologic motion, **earlier WB**, usually no removal, kam malreduction (kuch studies) | Knot/button prominence, skin irritation, osteolysis (rare), cost |
| **Both (hybrid)** | Kuch complex cases | Cost, evidence limited |
| **AITFL/PITFL repair/augmentation (InternalBrace)** | Biological repair | Evidence evolving |

> [!verify] Evidence flag: Kuch RCTs/meta-analyses suggest suture-button se **functional scores aur malreduction rate better** hain, par long-term evidence mixed hai. Dono acceptable hain.

**Surgical technique (key steps):**
1. **Fibula fracture hai to pehle fix karo** (length, rotation restore, kyunki isse syndesmosis reduction aasan hoti hai).
2. **Reduction clamp** se fibula ko tibial incisura mein reduce karo. **Ankle neutral/dorsiflexion mein** fix karo (plantarflexion mein fix karoge to **over-tightening** hogi aur dorsiflexion limit ho jaata hai).
3. **Level:** ~2-3 cm (kuch ~2-5 cm, flag) **joint line ke upar**, parallel to joint, 30° posterior-to-anterior angle (fibula ke posterior se tibia ke anteromedial).
4. Fluoroscopy: AP, mortise, lateral. **Malreduction** (fibula incisura mein ghum gayi, sagittal malrotation) lateral view mein check karo.
5. **Cotton test intraoperatively** (screw/button ke baad aur pehle) aur direct visualization agar possible.
6. **Posterior malleolus** fragment hai (>~25% ya unstable) to fix karo, ye syndesmosis stability improve karta hai.
7. **Deltoid:** syndesmosis fix ke baad medial clear space reduce na ho to deltoid interposition dhoondo.

**Malreduction = #1 reason for poor outcome** (CT se ~25-50% cases mein post-op malreduction mila hai kuch series mein, flag). **Intra-op CT/3D fluoro** consider karo.

**Post-op (general, surgeon-dependent):**
- Splint/boot, **NWB ~4-6 weeks (screw)** ya **earlier protected WB ~2-4 weeks (suture-button, flag)**.
- Rehab progression, ROM start jaldi, strengthening ~6-8 weeks, running ~**12-16 weeks**, sport ~**4-6 months**.
- **Screw removal:** 3-6 months baad kuch log karte hain agar symptomatic ya broken. Routine removal debatable (breakage kam hota hai aur removal ka fayda unclear).

## 8. Recovery timeline (patient ko bolne ke liye)
| Situation | Return to sport |
|---|---|
| Grade I | ~2-4 weeks |
| Grade II | ~4-8 weeks |
| Grade III/surgical | ~**3-6 months** |
| Fracture + syndesmosis fixation | ~4-6+ months |

**Ye lateral sprain se ~2x lamba hota hai**, aur dard dene ka "slow resolve" pattern hota hai. Patient ko pehle se bata do, warna wo samajhega ki kuch galat hai.

## 9. Complications
- **Chronic syndesmotic instability** (missed/undertreated)
- **Malreduction** → early arthritis
- **Heterotopic ossification/synostosis** (tibiofibular), stiffness
- **Hardware:** screw breakage, prominence, infection, suture-button osteolysis
- **Post-traumatic ankle arthritis**
- **Persistent pain**, OLT missed
- **Chronic/missed:** **syndesmotic reconstruction** (tendon graft) ya **arthrodesis** (rare)

## 10. Red flags / pitfalls
1. **Lateral sprain jaisa treat karna**: 6 weeks baad bhi pain, anterior-superior ankle dard, push-off pe dard.
2. **Proximal fibula tenderness** = Maisonneuve, poori tibia-fibula X-ray lo.
3. **Normal X-ray ≠ stable.** Stress/weight-bearing/MRI/CT lo.
4. **Medial clear space widening** = deltoid + syndesmosis ho sakta hai.
5. **Posterior malleolus fracture** = syndesmosis unstable ho sakta hai.
6. **Over-tightening** (plantarflexion mein fix) = dorsiflexion loss.
7. **Weight bearing ya sports jaldi** = diastasis badh sakta hai.
8. **Missed OLT** = persistent pain ka common reason.

## 11. Pharma/adjuncts
- Koi **specific drug nahi** syndesmosis healing ke liye. Pain ke liye paracetamol, short NSAID (PEACE & LOVE debate flag), topical NSAID.
- **Collagen/chondroitin supplements** (jo tune pichhle message mein poocha): evidence nahi, yahan bhi nahi.
- Post-op **DVT prophylaxis** (immobilisation, NWB), risk-based.

## 12. Quick OPD/OT rules {revise}
1. Twisting + dorsiflexion/ER + anterior-superior ankle pain = syndesmosis suspect karo.
2. **Palpate proximal fibula** har baar.
3. **Weight-bearing X-ray + (MRI/CT)** agar doubt ho, **normal X-ray pe bharosa mat karo.**
4. **Stable** = protected WB + rehab, **unstable** = surgery.
5. **Reduction quality** sabse important (malreduction = poor outcome).
6. Fix karte waqt **ankle dorsiflexion**, fibula incisura mein reduce, CT/3D se confirm.
7. **Patient counselling:** recovery lambi hai, 2x lateral sprain.
