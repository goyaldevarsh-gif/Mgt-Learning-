## Course ke sabse zaroori sabak
> [!tip] Ek nazar mein: 12 points jo poore course se yaad rakhne hain
> 1. **Treat the patient, not the X-ray.** Symptoms, exam aur imaging match karein, warna operate mat karo.
> 2. **Optimise before you operate:** sugar (HbA1c <8%), Hb >=13, eGFR, nutrition, smoking. Threshold = optimisation trigger, mana nahi.
> 3. **Knee:** joint line ~3° varus, VCA long-leg film se, resection = implant thickness, joint line change <~4 mm.
> 4. **Osteophytes pehle, ligament release baad mein.** Gaps rectangular aur extension = flexion.
> 5. **Posterior referencing = front pays (notch/overstuff), anterior referencing = flexion gap pays.**
> 6. **Constraint pre-op decide karo,** least constraint jo stable de, next-level implant ready rakho.
> 7. **Hip:** templating sirf true AP pelvis par, ~120% magnification, marker se confirm.
> 8. **Offset pehle, length baad mein.** High-offset stem ya liner, neck cut length decide karta hai.
> 9. **Stems:** polished taper-slip (subsidence intended) vs composite beam (no movement). Cementless: proximal fixation, stress shielding se bacho.
> 10. **Rehab:** cane opposite haath mein, stairs "good up, bad down", THA mein >90° flexion + adduction + IR avoid, TKA mein cross-legged avoid.
> 11. **Periprosthetic fracture:** stable stem = fix, loose stem = revise. 50% B1 asal mein B2.
> 12. **Robot ya brace koi bhi optimisation ya good surgery ka substitute nahi.**
