## 1. Design evolution

One question drives the whole story: **cruciates hata diye, to rollback kaun karega, aur constraint kitna theek hai?**

:::flow
[teal] Total condylar 1973 | Dono cruciates out. No rollback, flexion 90-100 deg [[p.406-407]]
v
Duopatellar / Kinematic condylar | PCL retained, tibial cutout [[p.407]]
v
Posterior-stabilized 1978 | Cam-post engages ~70 deg, gives rollback [[p.407-408]] ;; CR deep-dish | Dishing resists roll-forward [[p.409]]
v
[amber] CCK | Bigger post, deeper box [[p.410]]
v
[red] Rotating hinge | Salvage only [[p.412]]
:::

![Fig 7.1: Total condylar prosthesis (Insall, 1973)](figs/f7_1.jpg)

| Design | Key point | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Total condylar | Dished poly with A/P lips; criticised for no rollback and posterior subluxation if flexion gap > extension gap | 406-407 |
| CR | PCL gives rollback; least constraint; medial roll-forward | 409 |
| CR deep-dish | Dishing gives A/P stability; some sagittal laxity reports | 409 |
| PS | Cam-post replaces PCL; extra bone removal for box | 407-410 |
| CCK | Large post in deep box; small varus-valgus toggle; severe valgus, MCL deficiency, revision | 410 |
| Rotating hinge | Free rotation, blocks extension; ligament loss, recurvatum, limb salvage | 412 |
| Mobile bearing | No proven benefit over fixed; recent studies show higher revision | 411 |
| UKA | One compartment; high-volume centres do better | 411 |

> [!samajh] Constraint ka rule
> Constraint badhne se stability badhti hai, par **implant-bone interface pe stress bhi badhta hai** (loosening). Isliye hamesha *kam se kam constraint jo kaam kare*. CR, PS, CCK, hinge isi ladder pe hain.

> [!verify] Book mein dekho: CCK/mobile-bearing/UKA ke survival numbers (Maynard, Callaghan, Buechel) [[p.410-411]]

## 2. Knee kinematics

- Motion is **triaxial**, flexion axis is not fixed (J-shaped path) [[p.413]]
- **Medial pivot** (Dennis): medial condyle moves ~**2 mm**, lateral ~**21 mm** posteriorly in flexion. After ACL cut: 5 mm vs 17 mm [[p.413]]
- Explains screw-home: tibia externally rotates in extension, internally in flexion

| Activity | Flexion needed |
|---|---|
| Swing phase | 67 deg |
| Stairs up | 83 deg |
| Stairs down | 90 deg |
| Chair rise | 93 deg |

- **Forgiveness:** implant should tolerate slight malposition without overloading the interface [[p.414]]

> [!samajh] Medial pivot kaise yaad rakhein
> Medial side = **pivot** (kam move). Lateral side = **ghoomti** (zyada peeche). Isi se tibia flexion mein internally rotate hota hai.

## 3. PCL: retain or substitute?

![Fig 7.6: Roll-forward of medial femoral condyle after ~30 deg flexion in CR](figs/f7_6.jpg)

| Issue | Book says | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Results | CR ~ PS at 10-15 yrs. Mayo 15-yr survivorship **CR 90% vs PS 77%** | 414-416 |
| CR problem | Roll-forward medially; anterior lip raised to prevent | 414-415 |
| PS problem | More stress at implant-bone interface; patellar clunk | 415 |
| PCL release | Flexion gap **+4-6 mm**, extension gap **< 2 mm** | 415 |
| Tight PCL | Limits flexion, excess rollback, posterior poly wear | 415 |
| Low volume | Some authors: < 20 TKAs/yr, use PS | 415 |
| Proprioception | PCL mechanoreceptors survive in CR TKA (Fig 7.16) | 416 |
| Deformity | PCL is a **tether**; collateral release alone can fail | 416 |

> [!samajh] Patellar clunk (PS) ek line mein
> Patella ke upar ka **synovial nodule** flexion mein femoral box ke andar fasta hai, extension mein jhatke se nikalta hai = clunk. **Longer trochlear groove** se box ka edge door shuru hota hai. Fig 7.78 [[p.465-466]] mein dekho.

> [!samajh] Gap ka rule
> PCL cut karo = flexion gap zyada. Isliye PS mein extension gap bhi badhana padta hai (distal femur ~2 mm extra), aur joint line thodi upar ja sakti hai.

### Quick Q&A

:::qa PCL release se gaps kaise badalte hain?
Flexion gap +4-6 mm, extension gap < 2 mm (cadaver, non-arthritic knees) [[p.415]].
:::

:::qa PS ka patellar clunk kyon hota hai?
Quadriceps tendon ke peeche ka synovial nodule box mein fasta hai, extension mein jhatke se nikalta hai. Remedy: longer trochlear groove [[p.415]].
:::

:::qa PCL ka tether effect?
Contracted PCL deformity ko pakde rakhti hai. CR mein PCL balance/recess karni pad sakti hai, collateral release akela kaafi nahi [[p.416]].
:::

## 4. Axial and rotational alignment

![Fig 7.19: Femoral component in 5-7 deg valgus restores neutral mechanical axis](figs/f7_19.jpg)

| Rule | Value | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Femoral anatomic axis | Valgus 6 +/- 2 deg | 417 |
| Femoral valgus cut | **5-7 deg** | 417 |
| Tibial component | **Perpendicular** to mech axis; > 5 deg varus subsides into more varus | 417 |
| Femoral rotation | **~3 deg external** to posterior condylar axis | 418 |
| Campbell on kinematic alignment | Normal mechanical axis, joint line parallel to ground | 417 |
| Tibial rotation | Tray at medial 1/3 : lateral 2/3 junction of tubercle; check with trials through ROM | 418 |

![Fig 7.20: Externally rotating femoral component for a rectangular flexion space](figs/f7_20.jpg)

> [!samajh] Femur ko 3 deg ER kyon?
> Tibia perpendicular kati, jabki asli tibia ~3 deg varus hai. Bina rotation ke flexion gap **trapezoid** banega (medial tight). Femur ko ~3 deg ER karo to gap **rectangle** banta hai.

> [!flag] Valgus / hypoplastic lateral condyle: posterior condylar axis galat ho jaata hai. Epicondylar axis ya Whiteside AP axis use karo (Fig 7.42) [[p.440]]

:::qa Tibial component > 5 deg varus kyon avoid?
Wo aur zyada varus mein subside ho jaata hai [[p.417]].
:::

:::qa Kab posterior condylar axis pe bharosa nahi?
Worn posterior condyle ya hypoplastic lateral condyle (valgus knee). Tab epicondylar ya AP axis [[p.418]].
:::

## 5. Patellofemoral biomechanics

- Patella = pulley, **lengthens extensor lever arm**; greatest at **20-30 deg** flexion [[p.419]]
- Quadriceps force rises sharply in **last 20 deg** of extension
- **Q angle** (Hvid): bigger = more lateral subluxation tendency
- Modern femoral design: prominent lateral flange, **trochlear groove angle up to 7 deg** (Fig 7.23)
- **JRF:** 2-5 x BW in ADL, **7-8 x** in deep squat [[p.420]]

![Fig 7.24: Patellofemoral contact moves from inferior to superior with flexion](figs/f7_24.jpg)

| Flexion | Patellar contact |
|---|---|
| ~20 deg | Inferior surface touches trochlea |
| ~60 deg | Middle |
| ~90 deg | Superior (+ medial facet on medial condyle) |
| > 120 deg | Only medial and lateral edges |

> [!samajh] Isliye edge loading
> Contact flexion ke saath inferior se superior move karta hai, to poly patella pe eccentric load aur shear padta hai. Wahi se wear/loosening shuru hoti hai.

:::qa Extensor lever arm kab sabse zyada?
20-30 deg flexion [[p.419]].
:::

:::qa Patellar JRF squat mein?
7-8 x body weight (ADL mein 2-5 x) [[p.420]].
:::
