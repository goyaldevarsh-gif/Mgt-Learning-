Source reference: Rockwood & Wilkins' Fractures in Children, Campbell's Operative Orthopaedics (sports/pediatric chapters), Green's Pediatric Orthopaedics. Book ka text copy nahi kar sakta, to ye teeno ke content ka working summary hai. Chapter exact ke liye index mein "tibial eminence/spine fractures" dekh lena.

## 1. Basics
- **Age:** 8-14 yrs peak (cycling, sports fall). Rare in adults, and adults mein usually high-energy.
- **Why kids?** Tibial eminence incompletely ossified hota hai, to *bone/cartilage is weaker than the ACL*. Isliye ACL tootne ki jagah apni attachment ko kheench leta hai.
- **Mechanism:** hyperextension ± valgus/rotation, ya direct blow in flexion.
- **Adults:** associated injuries zaroor dhoondo (collateral, meniscus, PCL, plateau #).

## 2. Meyers & McKeever classification (modified by Zaricznyj)
| Type | Description | Samjho |
|---|---|---|
| I | Minimal/no displacement | Cast |
| II | Anterior 1/3-1/2 elevated, posterior hinge intact ("beak") | Reduce karke dekho |
| III | Completely displaced (IIIA: ACL fragment only, IIIB: whole eminence) | Surgery |
| IV | Comminuted/rotated | Surgery |

## 3. OPD assessment
- **History:** acute pain, swelling, bachcha weight nahi le raha.
- **Exam:** tense haemarthrosis, **extension block** (fragment ya meniscus phansa hai), Lachman/anterior drawer positive but pain mein unreliable. Collaterals, meniscus, NV status check karo.
- **X-ray:** AP + **true lateral** (displacement yahin dikhta hai). Oblique/tunnel view helpful.
- **CT:** comminution, fragment size aur planning ke liye.
- **MRI:** young child (mostly cartilage fragment), X-ray normal par haemarthrosis, ya meniscal entrapment/associated injury doubt ho.

## 4. Management algorithm
**Type I**
- Aspirate haemarthrosis (comfort + exam).
- Long leg cast/brace in extension to ~20° flexion, 4-6 weeks.
- **Repeat X-ray at ~1 week** (late displacement catch karne ke liye).

**Type II**
- Aspirate, then gentle closed reduction in extension (hyperextension force mat karo, full extension ya 0-20° flexion tak).
- Anatomical reduction confirm karo on X-ray, phir cast.
- Reduction na mile ya gap rahe, matlab meniscus/IM ligament interposed hai → surgery.

**Type III / IV / failed Type II → surgery (arthroscopic preferred)**

## 5. Surgery: step by step
1. Diagnostic arthroscopy, haematoma wash.
2. **Check for interposed tissue:** anterior horn of medial meniscus ya intermeniscal ligament fragment ke neeche phansa hota hai. Probe/traction suture se nikaalo. Yehi sabse common reason hai reduction fail hone ka.
3. Fracture bed debride karo (clot, fibrous tissue).
4. Reduce the fragment (probe ya guide se).
5. Fix:
   - **Screw (3.5-4.0 mm cannulated):** large, non-comminuted fragment, older child/adult. *Countersink karo* (warna ACL/notch impingement, extension loss). Epiphysis ke andar rakho, physis cross mat karo.
   - **Suture fixation:** high-strength sutures ACL ke through, transtibial tunnels, anterior tibia pe bone bridge ya button pe tie. Comminuted/small fragment aur skeletally immature mein preferred. Physis ko spare karta hai, aur hardware removal ki zarurat nahi.
   - **Suture anchors:** alternative.
6. Tie/fix at ~20-30° flexion. Fragment proud ya over-tight nahi hona chahiye (extension loss).
7. Open approach (medial parapatellar) sirf jab arthroscopic reduction na ho.

## 6. Post-op protocol (general)
- Brace in extension, protected weight bearing, 4-6 weeks (fixation stability ke hisaab se).
- Early controlled ROM, phir quadriceps/hamstring strengthening, proprioception.
- Return to sport ~4-6 months, jab strength and stability regain ho jaye.

## 7. Complications (parents ko pehle counsel karo)
- **Arthrofibrosis / loss of extension**, sabse common.
- **Residual ACL laxity** union ke baad bhi common hai, par aksar clinically symptomatic nahi hoti. Parents ko pehle bata do.
- Nonunion/malunion with impingement.
- Hardware prominence/irritation.
- Growth disturbance (rare, screw physis cross kare to).
- Persistent instability ho to baad mein ACL reconstruction.

## 8. Adult-specific pearls
- **Segond fracture** (lateral tibial plateau avulsion) → almost pathognomonic of ACL tear. Miss mat karna.
- Adult mein fragment bada aur bone strong hai, to screw ya suture dono work karte hain.
- Neglected displaced fragment ya symptomatic laxity → ACL reconstruction.
- **Midsubstance ACL tear** alag entity hai. Skeletally immature mein physeal-sparing ya transphyseal reconstruction (age/maturity ke hisaab se).

## OPD/OT quick rules {revise}
1. Child + haemarthrosis + extension block = tibial eminence fracture suspect karo.
2. Hamesha lateral X-ray dekho.
3. Type I/II ke baad repeat X-ray zaroor.
4. Reduction nahi mil raha = meniscus phansa hai, scope karo.
5. Fix karo flexion mein, countersink karo, physis cross mat karo.

## Samajh: Type II closed reduction, extension mein kyun {revise}

> [!samajh] Type II mein fragment aage se uthha hai, peeche hinge intact. Extension mein femoral condyle fragment ko neeche dabata hai, to wo apne bed mein baith sakta hai.

**Closed reduction ka logic:** Type II mein fragment aage se uthha hota hai (beak), peeche hinge intact. Knee ko **extension** mein laane se femoral condyle fragment ko neeche dabata hai, to wo apne bed mein baith sakta hai.

**Hyperextension kyun nahi:** Purane texts mein "full hyperextension mein reduce karo" likha milta hai. Problem ye hai ki hyperextension se ACL aur fragment pe aur strain aata hai, aur popliteal NV structures pe bhi tension aata hai. Aur isse reduction ka fayda nahi, risk zyada hai.

**Practical tarika:**
1. Pehle haemarthrosis aspirate karo (pain kam, exam aasan).
2. Knee ko slowly **full extension tak ya 0-20° flexion** mein laao. Zabardasti hyperextension mat karo.
3. Lateral X-ray (ya fluoroscopy) mein dekho ki fragment bed mein baith gaya ya nahi.
4. Baith gaya to isi position mein long leg cast/brace.
5. Gap bacha ya fragment nahi baitha → meniscus/IM ligament phansa hai → scope karo.

Ye bhi dhyan rakho: kuch authors ke hisaab se Type II mein bhi extension mein reduction aksar incomplete rehta hai, isliye imaging pe bharosa karo, sirf clinical feel pe nahi.
## Viva {viva}

:::qa Bachon mein ACL avulsion kyun hota hai?
Tibial eminence incompletely ossified hota hai, bone/cartilage ACL se weak hai, isliye ACL attachment ko kheench leta hai.
:::

:::qa Classification?
Meyers & McKeever (I, II, III), Zaricznyj ne type IV (comminuted/rotated) joda.
:::

:::qa Reduction nahi mil raha to?
Anterior horn of medial meniscus ya intermeniscal ligament interposed hota hai. Scope karo, probe/traction suture se nikalo.
:::

:::qa Sabse common complication aur counselling?
Arthrofibrosis/extension loss. Residual ACL laxity union ke baad bhi common hai, parents ko pehle bata do.
:::

:::qa Segond fracture kya hai?
Lateral tibial plateau avulsion, almost pathognomonic of ACL tear.
:::
