## 1. Outcome scores

| Score | Gist | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Knee Society Score 2011** | Objective knee score 100 (alignment 25, stability 25, ROM 25, symptoms 25) + Satisfaction 40 + Expectation 15 + Functional activity 100 | 432 |
| **LEAS** | Patient picks highest activity level from a graded list; validated vs WOMAC/pedometer | 432 |
| **KOOS JR** | 1-page, 7 items: pain, symptoms, ADL | 432 |
| **KS radiographic (1989)** | Alignment, tibial coverage, radiolucencies, patellar list | 433 |

- 2011 KSS stresses **patient perception**: patient and surgeon opinion differ [[p.432]]
- Tibial radiolucency (7 zones): **<= 4 non-progressive** = probably insignificant, **5-9** = close follow-up, **>= 10** = possible/impending failure [[p.433]]

## 2. Survival

- Original total condylar: **95% at 15 yrs, 91% at 21-23 yrs** [[p.432]]
- CR and PS designs: 10-yr survivorship **>= 95%**, consistent with registries

| Why TKA is revised (Sharkey) | % |
|---|---|
| Loosening | 39.9 |
| Infection | 27.4 |
| Instability | 7.5 |
| Periprosthetic fracture | 4.7 |
| Arthrofibrosis | 4.5 |

> [!tip] **Early revision (< 2 yrs) = infection. Late = aseptic loosening.** Viva mein pakka poochte hain.

Objective knee score (100), satisfaction (40), expectations (15), functional activity (100) [[p.432]].

## 3. Pre-op evaluation

- **Confirm indication first** [[p.434]]
- **X-rays:** standing AP, lateral, skyline + **long-leg standing AP** (mechanical axis; shows tibial bowing that blocks an IM tibial guide)
- **Cardiopulmonary reserve** for **1000-1500 mL** blood loss; ECG routine; chest X-ray only if cardiopulmonary history; coag tests only if bleeding history

## 4. Anaesthesia and pain

- **Regional > general** for primary TKA, **spinal preferred** (AAOS) [[p.435]]
- **Multimodal:** pre-op COX-2 + gabapentin; local infiltration (bupivacaine/ropivacaine); femoral catheters under scrutiny (delay mobilisation) [[p.436]]
- Up to **40%** become chronic narcotic users: wean them
- **PILLAR:** 20 mL liposomal bupivacaine + 20 mL 0.5% bupivacaine + 80 mL saline

## 5. Tranexamic acid (TXA)

| Route | Dose |
|---|---|
| **IV** | 10-15 mg/kg or **1 g**, 20 min before tourniquet inflation, repeat ~15 min before deflation |
| **Topical** | 1.5-3 g in 100 mL saline, in the wound for 5 min before deflation |
| **Oral** | 2000 mg 2 h pre-op, then 4-6 h and 12 h after |

> [!flag] IV TXA avoid: clotting/bleeding disorder, SAH, PE, DVT, CVA, MI, coronary stent [[p.436]]
