## 1. Special acetabular liners

| Option | What it does | Use / catch | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elevated-rim / lateralised liner | Rim lip or up to 20 deg reorientation, or lateralises centre | Can **cause** impingement if lip is misplaced; used less with big heads | 195 |
| **Constrained** liner | Locks the head in (tripolar or locking ring) | Deficient abductors, soft tissue, neuromuscular, recurrent dislocation. **Less ROM, impingement failure.** Never to rescue a malpositioned shell; avoid skirted heads | 195 |
| **Dual mobility** | Unconstrained tripolar: small head inside large poly ball inside polished shell | Bigger effective head, **no ROM loss**. Dislocation **0.88%** primary (Combes), 1.5% revision. Risk: intraprosthetic dislocation | 195 |
| Custom / 3-D printed triflange | CT-based, flanges + screws | Massive defects only; most defects now use hemispherical cup + **porous metal augments** | 195 |
| Antiprotrusio ring / cage | Spans defect, fixed to ilium + ischium | Poor long-term biologic fixation, fracture/loosening; **cup-cage** adds biologic fixation | 195-196 |

> [!samajh] Constrained vs dual mobility
> Dono stability ke liye, par **constrained = ROM kam, impingement force implant pe**; **dual mobility = ROM bachta hai**. Dono technical error (galat cup position) ko cover nahi karte.

## 2. Bearing surfaces

### Highly crosslinked polyethylene (HXLPE) [[p.197-199]]

- Conventional UHMWPE sterilised with 2.5 Mrad. **HXLPE:** 5-10 Mrad, then **remelting** (kills free radicals but loses crystallinity, toughness) or **annealing** (keeps strength, less effective). **Vitamin E doping** scavenges radicals without remelting
- Simulator wear drop **80-90%**
- Wear in vivo: steady state **0.003 mm/yr** (Glyn-Jones, RSA, 10 yrs); osteolysis threshold **0.1 mm/yr**
- Australian registry 16-yr revision: **6.2% HXLPE vs 11.7%** conventional
- Wear not related to head size (22-46 mm) but **thin liner** + large head = fatigue, delamination, fracture (Tower: 4 liner fractures)
- Liners fit existing shells: **liner exchange** possible without shell revision

### Ceramic-on-ceramic [[p.199]]

| Point | Book |
|---|---|
| Wear | Alumina-on-alumina **4000 x** less than CoCr-on-poly; < 0.025 mm/yr at 18.5 yrs |
| Head fracture | **Delta ceramic ~1 in 100,000 vs alumina ~1 in 5000**. More with small heads and short necks |
| Problems | Rim chipping, **stripe wear**, **squeaking** (> 10% in some series; Blakeney 23% with big delta heads), edge loading with vertical cups |
| Practical | Careful liner orientation (chipping at insertion); limited neck lengths, no skirts: template well |

### Others

- **Oxidized zirconium:** ~5 µm ceramic layer integral to metal, no nickel (metal allergy), chip-resistant; **heads only, with poly** [[p.199-200]]
- **Metal-on-metal (large head):** largely abandoned (ALTR); **resurfacing** still an option in young men [[p.197]]
- **Trunnionosis** (fretting corrosion at trunnion with CoCr head): more common than thought, so more ceramic/ceramicised heads now. Large databases: ceramic bearings may have lower infection risk (reason unclear) [[p.200]]

> [!samajh] Bearing choose kaise
> Young + active: **HXLPE on ceramic/CoCr head** dominant. Ceramic-ceramic = lowest wear par **noise, chipping, position sensitivity**. MoM = ALTR ki wajah se band.

:::qa HXLPE ke 2 manufacturing routes?
Remelting (free radicals gone, strength down) vs annealing (strength kept, less effective); vitamin E doping avoids remelting [[p.197]].
:::

:::qa Ceramic head fracture risk kab zyada?
Small heads (28 mm), short necks, worn trunnion [[p.199]].
:::

## 3. Indications and contraindications

- **Main indication:** pain from degenerative or destructive hip disease on imaging, **failed conservative care** (weight loss, analgesics, activity change, low-impact exercise, aids) [[p.200]]
- **Limp, stiffness or short leg with little pain = not a candidate** (authors)
- NIH 1994: THR is an option for nearly all with chronic hip disease and major functional loss
- Young patients: consider **femoral / periacetabular osteotomy**, core decompression, FAI surgery, arthrodesis, resurfacing first [[p.200]]
- **Bilateral THA at once:** only fit patient with bilateral severe disease and stiffness/FFD. **PDA or septal defect = absolute contraindication**
- **Absolute contraindications:** active hip or other infection; unstable medical illness. **Asymptomatic bacteriuria is not a contraindication** [[p.200]]

## 4. Pre-op evaluation and optimisation

:::flow
[teal] Modifiable risks | Fix before surgery [[p.201-202]]
v
Antiplatelets | stop 7-10 d ;; Smoking | stop >= 6 wks (cotinine) ;; HbA1c | > 7.5% risk, 7.5-8 upper limit
v
Obesity | BMI 40 cut; infection rises with BMI ;; Nasal S. aureus | decolonise ;; Anaemia | Hb < 13 men, < 12 women
v
BMI < 18.5 | infection risk ;; Albumin, prealbumin, TLC | malnutrition ;; Skin, dental, urinary | clear first
:::

- Warfarin/Xa inhibitors stopped until coagulation normal; **bridge** with enoxaparin
- **Stents:** if clopidogrel stopped, continue aspirin; coordinate with cardiology
- Anaemia management: iron, EPO, **TXA**, blood-management protocol
- Prophylaxis doses (Box 3.2): **cefazolin 2 g** (3 g if >= 129 kg), redose 4 h; **vancomycin 15-20 mg/kg**; clindamycin 600-900 mg, redose 6 h [[p.202]]
- Exam: **Trendelenburg**, limb lengths; **adduction contracture = apparent shortening**, abduction contracture = apparent lengthening; **FFD** pushes the lumbar spine into lordosis [[p.202]]
- **Hip and knee both bad: hip first** (book) [[p.202]]
- **True hip pain:** groin / lateral hip / anterior thigh / knee. **Buttock pain = think spine/SI joint** [[p.202]]
- Scores: **Harris** (most used), WOMAC, SF-36, **HOOS Jr** (6 questions), VR-12, PROMIS [[p.202]]

> [!samajh] Hip pain ka rule
> Groin = hip. Buttock/posterior pelvis = pehle spine. Spine ka pain THA se nahi jaata.

## 5. Pre-op radiographs and templating

- Minimum: **AP pelvis** + **lateral hip**. Previous acetabular fracture: **obturator and iliac oblique**, 3-D CT. Narrow canal in dysplasia/dwarfism; bowing in Paget [[p.203]]

### Templating (Technique 3.1, Capello) [[p.203-204]]

:::flow
[teal] AP pelvis, hips 15 deg internal rotation | Magnification marker (spheres 100 mm apart); ~20% [[p.203]]
v
Limb-length difference | Line through ischial tuberosities (or interteardrop) to lesser trochanters
v
Acetabular template | Medial at **teardrop**, inferior at obturator foramen: centre = new centre of rotation
v
Femoral template | Best canal fill; allow for cement mantle; choose neck length for LLD and offset
v
Mark neck-cut level | Distance above lesser trochanter, use as intra-op reference
:::

- External rotation **underestimates offset**. Fixed ER deformity: templating inaccurate. Template the **normal side** as a check
- Interteardrop line more accurate than ischium (Meerman)
- Digital templating accurate; a magnification marker did not improve accuracy over standard 20% (Archibeck)

## 6. Hip-spine relationship

- **Spinal fusion:** 2 x early dislocation, > 3 x revision. Most early dislocations are in **Lewinnek safe zone** cups, so the safe zone is not universal [[p.204]]
- Standing to sitting: pelvis tilts **posteriorly**; each 1 deg of tilt changes anteversion by **0.7-0.8 deg** (about **15.6 deg** total), reducing anterior impingement
- **Sacral slope (SS)** change normally **11-30 deg**; **<= 10 deg = spinopelvic stiffness**
- **Pelvic incidence (PI)**: fixed, does not change with posture

| Patient | Problem | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Stiff spine** (fusion, degenerative) | Hip must flex more, **anterior impingement** sitting | **More** cup anteversion |
| **Flatback** (high posterior tilt standing) | Functional anteversion too high standing, **anterior instability** | **Less** cup anteversion |

- Needs standing + sitting **lateral lumbar-pelvis** films (minimum). **Dual mobility** helps; EOS imaging [[p.205]]

> [!samajh] Yaad rakhne ka tareeka
> **Stiff spine = pelvis hil nahi pata = hip ko zyada flex karna padta hai = anterior impingement = zyada anteversion chahiye.** Flatback mein ulta.

:::qa Spinopelvic stiffness ka definition?
Change in sacral slope <= 10 deg between standing and sitting (normal 11-30 deg) [[p.204]].
:::

## 7. Positioning and draping

- Tilting table, **secure** pelvic positioners placed on pubis/ASIS (not on femoral triangles: ischemia, neuropathy); pad peroneal nerve and bony prominences [[p.205]]
- Positioner errors still **misjudge cup anteversion**
- Supine: pad under the buttock; impervious drapes; U-drape seals perineum
